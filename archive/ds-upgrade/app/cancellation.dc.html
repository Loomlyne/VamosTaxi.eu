01What you get back

Measured from the pickup time in your confirmation, not from the time you booked.

More than

24 hours before pickup

100% refunded

Refunded to the card you paid with. Or take the full value as a voucher instead — see section 09.

Inside

24 hours before pickup

75% refunded

A driver is already committed to your journey by this point, which is what the retained share covers.

After pickup time

No-show, or not cancelled

No refund

The vehicle waited. Section 06 sets out when a booking counts as a no-show.

Vehicles over 8 seats work to a longer window of 72 hours, because a coach cannot be re-sold at short notice.

03Changing a booking

Time, address, passenger count and vehicle class can be changed up to Modification deadline before pickup, free of charge. If the change moves the fare, you pay or we refund the difference — never more than the difference.

A change requested after that deadline is treated as a cancellation and a new booking. It is worth calling us first: if a driver can still be moved, we will move them.

04Flight delays

Give us the flight number and a delay costs you nothing. We track the flight and the driver arrives for the actual landing time; the included waiting allowance starts from then, not from the time you booked.

  • Delay within the same day: the pickup moves automatically. No call needed.
  • Flight cancelled or diverted: tell us as soon as you know and we treat it under section 07, not as a late cancellation.
  • No flight number on the booking: we cannot see the delay, so the ordinary waiting rules in the terms apply.

05If no driver arrives

Call the number in your confirmation before you make another arrangement, so we have a chance to fix it.

Where our records show you were at the pickup point and no vehicle came, you receive Driver noshow share back.

Client legal text · consequential costs

Must say whether anything beyond the fare is covered when a driver does not arrive — a replacement taxi, a missed flight — and what evidence is needed. This is the claim customers pursue hardest, and it has to agree with the liability section of the terms.

06No-show

A booking becomes a no-show once the included waiting time has run out and we have called your number Noshow call attempts times with no answer.

  • Airport pickups: Airport waiting from the actual landing time.
  • All other pickups: Standard waiting from the booked time.

Answer the phone and the driver waits — the extra time is chargeable at Extra waiting rate, which is cheaper than losing the transfer.

07Snow, strikes, closures

Where a journey becomes impossible or unsafe through something neither of us controls, we do not treat it as your cancellation. We re-plan first; if the journey cannot happen at all, the money is dealt with under this section rather than under the tiers above.

Client legal text · disruption outcome

Must state the outcome — refund, voucher, or re-planning at no cost — and how long a booking stays open before it lapses. It has to match force majeure in the terms; write it once, cross-link it twice.

08How refunds are paid

  • Always to the original payment method. We cannot redirect a refund to a different card or account.
  • Approved within Refund decision days of your cancellation confirmation.
  • Paid within Refund payout days days. Your bank may take a few days more to show it.
  • Bookings paid with a voucher are refunded as a voucher.

Unhappy with the outcome? The complaints route is in the terms, and we answer within Complaint resolution days days.

09Vouchers

Instead of a refund you can take the full value of the booking as a voucher, valid for Voucher validity. It is worth more than the refunded share, which is why we offer it.

  • Usable on any route we serve, by anyone you pass it to.
  • Redeemable against a higher fare — you pay the difference.
  • Not exchangeable for cash once issued.

Client legal text · voucher terms

Must state validity, transferability, part-redemption and what happens on expiry. Swiss law limits how short an expiry can be, so this needs checking rather than choosing.
