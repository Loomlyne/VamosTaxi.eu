A2 · the refund shares are still two different promises

The archived FAQ promised a full refund up to 24 hours; the archived terms grant 75% and retain 25%. Both were live promises to the same customer, so this page states neither — the share is a labelled gap in the cancel review and again in the cancelled state.

Four values are needed before this ships: the free-cancel window, the full share, the late-cancel share and the no-show share. The cancellation policy governs all of them; this page must quote it, never restate it.

Refunds are never automatic, and the copy says so

Nothing here says “refunded”. Cancelling stands the driver down immediately, which the copy states plainly; the money is described as eligible, reviewed by a person and paid within a stated window. The share is shown before the confirm button, not only after it.

Open thread · does an authenticated equivalent also ship?

If /account/booking-detail and /account/manage-cancel ship alongside this, they should be this page with the gate removed and the trip list around it — not a second design. The gate, the fork and all six outcome states are built to be reused that way.

Out of scope · what is deliberately absent

No lookup or magic-link verification, no Stripe refund call, no email sending, no admin-side refund execution. The change request ends as a request; nothing on this page moves money or writes to a booking.

The bookingTiming tweak is what decides whether the change is open or blocked and which refund tier is quoted — one flag stands in for two separate thresholds, because the modification deadline and the free-cancel window are independent tokens and may not turn out to be the same number.
