02Scope

These terms cover pre-booked private transfers arranged through vamostaxi.eu: airport pickups and drop-offs, city and long-distance journeys, and chauffeur bookings by the hour. They apply from the moment you confirm a booking.

We do not operate a street-hail or on-demand service. Every journey is booked ahead for a stated time, at a price agreed before you pay.

Not covered by this document

  • How we handle your personal data — see the privacy policy.
  • Cookies and consent — see the cookie policy.
  • Cancellations, changes and refunds — see the cancellation & refund policy, which governs.

03How a booking is formed

You enter the route, the date and the time, and the number of passengers and bags. We show a price for each vehicle class. You pay. We send a written confirmation with a booking reference.

The booking exists once that confirmation reaches you. Until then nothing is reserved and nothing is charged.

What the confirmation contains

  • Booking reference, pickup time and pickup address.
  • Vehicle class, passenger and luggage count.
  • The price you paid, in Swiss francs.
  • How to change or cancel the booking.

Driver name, vehicle and telephone number are sent by SMS and email Driver details lead time before pickup. Ask us and we will send them sooner.

04Fixed price

The price shown at the last step is the price you pay. It is quoted in Swiss francs and it does not move afterwards — not for traffic, not for a longer route than expected, not for a busy evening.

Included

  • The journey between the two addresses you gave us, and the driver's time for it.
  • Waiting time as set out in section 08.
  • Tolls, road charges and airport access fees.
  • The gratuity. Tipping is not expected on top.

Charged separately, only if you ask for it

  • Additional stops: Extra stop fee per stop.
  • Waiting beyond the included allowance: Extra waiting rate.
  • Extended city stay, maximum City stay minutes minutes: City stay fee.
  • Oversized or unusual items declared at booking: Oversize item fee.

Currency

05Partner carriers

Some journeys are driven by a partner carrier working to our standards rather than by our own vehicle. You are told the vehicle class at booking; the driver's details arrive before pickup.

Client legal text · subcontracting

Must state that we may use partner carriers, what standard they are held to, who carries the licence and insurance, and who the passenger complains to. Must agree with section 01 and section 13.

06Your obligations

  • Give an address the driver can reach, and a mobile number you will answer on the day.
  • Be at the pickup point at the agreed time.
  • Tell us the flight number for an airport pickup, so a delay is visible to us.
  • Declare passengers and bags honestly — a vehicle is assigned on those numbers.
  • Wear a seatbelt, and keep to Swiss road law.

Smoking is not permitted in our vehicles. Alcohol and food only with the driver's agreement.

Client legal text · refusal of carriage & soiling

Must state when a driver may refuse or end a journey, and what a passenger is liable for if a vehicle is damaged or soiled. A cleaning charge needs a figure or a stated method.

07Luggage & child seats

Every booked seat carries one medium case of Case dimensions and one piece of hand luggage. A vehicle is assigned on the count you gave us, so tell us if it changes.

Declare in advance

Skis and snowboards, folding bicycles, collapsible wheelchairs, musical instruments, pets. We will confirm the vehicle class that takes them.

Children

Child and booster seats are available on request. You may fit your own. Swiss law decides which seat a child needs, and the driver will not travel without it.

Client legal text · refused and unaccompanied items

Must state what may not be carried, and what happens to luggage left in a vehicle.

08Waiting time

Waiting is included, measured from the pickup time in your confirmation.

  • Airport pickups: Airport waiting, counted from the actual landing time we see against your flight number.
  • All other pickups: Standard waiting, counted from the booked time.

Beyond the allowance we charge Extra waiting rate. If your flight is delayed, the allowance moves with the flight — you do not need to call us.

09No-show

A booking counts as a no-show when the waiting allowance in section 08 has run out and we have called your number Noshow call attempts times without an answer.

A no-show is not refunded. What we hold on record as evidence is set out in the privacy policy.

If the driver does not arrive

Call us on the number in your confirmation. Where we can see that you were at the pickup point and no vehicle came, the cancellation & refund policy sets out what you get back.

10Changes & cancellation

Change or cancel from the link in your confirmation email, or from your account if you made one. A change is effective when we confirm it in writing, not when you send it.

One source of truth

Windows, refund shares, modification deadlines and flight-delay handling live in the cancellation & refund policy. That document governs. This section stays short on purpose, so the two can never drift apart.

13Liability

Client legal text · liability

The one section that must be drafted, not adapted. It has to sit with the answer to section 01: an intermediary and a carrier carry different liability. Consequential loss — a missed flight, a missed meeting — needs to be addressed explicitly, because that is what customers claim for.

What we do say plainly, whatever the drafting: we do not exclude liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, and nothing in these terms removes rights Swiss consumer law gives you.

14Force majeure

Snow, closed roads, strikes, an airport shutdown: events outside anyone's control can stop a journey. Where that happens we will get you moving if it is safe and possible, and the cancellation & refund policy says what happens to the money.

Client legal text · definition and consequences

Must define the events and state the consequence — suspension, re-planning, or refund — and how long the position holds before the booking lapses.
