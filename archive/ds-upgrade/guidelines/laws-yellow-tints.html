Out of the palette

--vt-yellow-50 / 100 / 200 / 300 as a background or border — cream panels, pale icon tiles, tinted alert strips.

Brownish, not gold — --vt-yellow-600 / 700 as text or icon colour.

In the palette

Book a transfer Assigned

Attention is carried by charcoal, by full-strength yellow on a charcoal ground, or by the semantic --vt-danger / --vt-success — never by a wash of pale yellow.

Kit components whose own default is tinted — Alert tone="accent", ListRow icon=…, Badge tone="warning" — use tone="inverse" / tone="info" instead.
