02What we collect

Only what a transfer needs, plus what the law makes us keep. We do not buy data about you and we do not build advertising profiles.

When you get a price

Pickup and drop-off address, date and time, passenger and luggage count. This is enough to price a journey and nothing here identifies you.

When you book

  • Lead passenger name, email address and mobile number.
  • Pickup and drop-off address, and any additional stop.
  • Flight number, where you gave one, and the arrival time we read from it.
  • Passenger and luggage count, child seat requests, declared items.
  • Anything you type into the notes field for the driver.

Your account. When you book, we create an account for your email address so you can see and manage your bookings. It has no password. To sign in, we send a link to your email. We keep your name, email, phone number and bookings in it. You can close your account at any time by writing to info@vamostaxi.site. We then delete what we are not required by law to keep.

When you pay

Payment metadata only: the amount, the currency, the method, the last four digits, and whether it succeeded. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's own form and never reach our systems.

While you use the site

Server logs — page, timestamp, referrer, browser, operating system and IP address — and whatever the cookie categories you allowed are set to collect. See the cookie policy.

When you contact us

Your message and the channel it came in on, kept with the booking it refers to.

03Why we may use it

To perform the contractTaking a booking, assigning a driver, collecting payment, sending your confirmation and driver details, handling a change or a cancellation. Legal basis under the GDPR: Art. 6 (1) (b).
Legal obligationAccounting, tax, and the trip records Swiss archiving law requires us to hold — see section 06.
Legitimate interestFraud prevention, service quality, evidence of a no-show, and defending a claim.
ConsentAnalytics and marketing cookies, and any newsletter. You can withdraw it at any time without giving a reason.

Client legal text · statutory references

Your adviser adds the article references for each basis under revFADP and, where a customer is in the EU, the GDPR. The layout holds one reference line per row without changing.

05Transfers abroad

Some of these suppliers process data outside Switzerland. Where that happens we rely on the transfer mechanism named in the row above, and we tell you which country.

Client legal text · transfer mechanism

One current mechanism per destination — an adequacy decision, or standard contractual clauses with the Swiss addendum. The archived policy relies on the EU–US Privacy Shield, which stopped being a valid basis in 2020; it cannot be carried over.

07Your rights

Two things are true at once, and this page shows them side by side rather than burying one of them.

You can ask us to

  • Give you a copy of what we hold
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete what we are not required to keep
  • Restrict or object to a particular use
  • Send your data to another provider
  • Withdraw a consent you gave

We have to keep

  • Trip records for 10 years
  • Payment and tax records
  • The record of your cookie choice
  • Evidence relating to an open claim

So a deletion request does not erase a journey we are legally obliged to archive. What we can delete, we delete. What we cannot, we tell you about, and we say which rule keeps it and when it expires.

You can also complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner in Bern, and — if you are in the EU — to your national authority. We would rather you came to us first.

When this policy changes we update the date and the version at the top of the page. If a change affects what you agreed to, we ask you again rather than assuming.
