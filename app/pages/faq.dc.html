Service & safety

Two questions

Every transfer is booked ahead and assigned to a named driver. You are not waiting for a car to be found — at the agreed time it is already there.

Included at the airport

Meet & greet

Your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a name sign, helps with the bags and walks you to the car. We watch the flight, so a delay moves the pickup without you doing anything. Available at Zurich (ZRH) and Geneva (GVA) airports.

You get their name by email as soon as a driver is assigned, and their direct number on your manage-booking page. Call or message them directly on the day.
