About Vamos Taxi

A Zurich operator, not a marketplace

You book a day ahead, you see the price before you pay, and at the agreed time the car is already there. That is the whole product. No surge, no meter, no waiting to find out what it costs.

Who we are

Founder-led, and answerable

Vamos Taxi GmbH was registered in Dietikon in the Canton of Zurich and is run by its owner, Ben Othman Houssein. When something goes wrong on a transfer, the person who answers is accountable for it — that is the advantage of being this size, and we would rather keep it.

{{ sOperate }} Every driver who works with us is licensed for passenger transport and insured for it, and we do not put a vehicle on a job we would not put a family member in.

We are a scheduled operator. You cannot hail us on the street and there is no app watching a car crawl towards you. What you get instead is a named driver, a confirmed time and a price that was agreed before anyone set off.

What you are buying

Four things we will hold to

The price does not move

What you see before you pay is what you pay. Traffic, a longer route than expected, a busy evening — none of it changes the number. Tolls, airport fees and the gratuity are already in it.

We watch your flight

Give us the flight number and the pickup follows the actual landing time. A delay does not need a phone call and does not cost you anything. Your driver meets you in arrivals with a name sign.

The car is there first

Booked ahead means the vehicle is assigned before the day, not searched for on it. Driver name, plate and number reach you Driver details lead time before pickup.

A real company behind it

Registered in the commercial register of the Canton of Zurich under CH-020.4.077.792-7, with a named managing director and an address you can write to. It is all on the imprint.

The fleet

Three classes, chosen by what you are carrying

Where we drive

Zurich first, and the routes that leave it

Airport transfers to and from Zurich are the core of the business. Beyond that we run the long-distance routes people actually ask for: the ski resorts in winter, Geneva and the other Swiss cities year round, and cross-border runs into France and Italy.

We do not claim to be everywhere. If a route is outside what we cover, we say so when you ask rather than after you have paid.
