About Vamos Taxi

A Zurich operator, not a marketplace

You book a day ahead, you see the price before you pay, and at the agreed time the car is already there. That is the whole product. No surge, no meter, no waiting to find out what it costs.

Who we are

Founder-led, and answerable

Vamos Taxi GmbH was registered in Dietikon in the Canton of Zurich and is run by its owner, Ben Othman Houssein. When something goes wrong on a transfer, the person who answers is accountable for it — that is the advantage of being this size, and we would rather keep it.

{{ sOperate }} Every driver who works with us is licensed for passenger transport and insured for it, and we do not put a vehicle on a job we would not put a family member in.

We are a scheduled operator. You cannot hail us on the street and there is no app watching a car crawl towards you. What you get instead is a named driver, a confirmed time and a price that was agreed before anyone set off.
