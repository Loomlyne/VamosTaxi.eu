Small files a site stores in your browser, plus the equivalents — local storage and pixels. We group them by what they are for, not by the technology.

Nothing except the strictly necessary category is set before you choose. If you never choose, nothing else is ever set.

07Changing your mind

Open your preferences from the link in the footer of every page, or from section 02 above. Turning a category off stops it immediately; anything already collected is handled as set out in the privacy policy.

Your browser settings also work, and they override anything we do. Blocking the strictly necessary category there will stop a booking from completing.

Change your mind any time

Your choice is a preference, not a contract. Reopen the panel whenever you like — no explanation needed.
