# Wording for Google Analytics 4 (adopted 2026-10-11)

**Status: ADOPTED as written** by the owner in chat, 2026-10-11 19:02 +04 ("adopt the GA4 wording as written"), including the consent version bump. Drafted by the agent; no lawyer has read it. Not on the site yet: the build copies it byte for byte into `app/vamos-meta-texts.js` and `apps/web/i18n/messages/*.json`, and `CONSENT_POLICY_VERSION` moves to the ship day (every visitor is asked again). Never reword without his adoption.

## Where GA4 sits

Under the existing **Analytics** choice in the cookie banner (today: Cloudflare Web Analytics, cookieless), not under Marketing. A visitor who accepts Marketing but not Analytics gets no GA4. A visitor who accepts Analytics but not Marketing gets GA4 with `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and `ad_personalization` denied (no advert use).

## Facts the texts rely on (the build must make them true)

| Fact | Build must |
|---|---|
| Cookies `_ga` and `_ga_<id>` on our domain | set nothing else of ours; the intercepted test run lists every cookie |
| Kept for up to 90 days (agent's proposal: same as the Meta and Google Ads rows; Google's default is 2 years) | `gtag('config', G-…, { cookie_expires: 7776000 })` |
| Loaded only after Analytics is accepted, asked from `/api/consent/state` like the Google Ads tag | `app/vamos-google.js` loads nothing before; withdraw deletes both cookies |
| Sent: page address without query, hash or booking reference; the previous site's bare origin; the same button and booking-step events as Meta; for a paid booking the amount and booking reference | no name, email, phone, route, flight, typed text |
| Google retention setting in GA4 Admin set to 2 months (shortest) | owner step in analytics.google.com |
| Google signals off, ads personalisation off in GA4 Admin | owner step |
