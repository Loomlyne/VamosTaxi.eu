# 2026-10-15 Google Tag Manager off

Owner decision, 2026-10-15.

## What the live check showed

Google Tag Manager container GTM-KPKGT4VH (the agency's) loaded its own gtag Google Ads AW-368142830 tag and GA4 G-K5MZME44P6 tag (both with `cx=c`) and set the cookie `_ga_JW17PMTRE9` (another GA4 property). These duplicate the tags the site already starts itself.

## Decision

The owner has no access to the container and asked us to fix it from our side.

- `GTM_ON` (app/vamos-google.js) and `GTM_OPEN` (apps/web/lib/google/flag.ts) are `false`. No gtm.js request, no noscript frame.
- The four adopted Tag Manager texts (banner sentence, cookies row, privacy line, processor row; `.planning/decisions/2026-10-13-gtm-wording.md`) are kept unchanged in app/vamos-meta-texts.js, the Next messages and the seed. They are rendered only while the flag is on (`GTM_SHOWN` in vamos-meta-texts.js for the mock, `GTM_OPEN` for the Next pages). A test pins the three flags together.
- Consent version stays `2026-10-13`: we process less, nobody needs re-asking.

## Turn it back on

Only when the container holds no Google Ads, GA4 or Meta tag. Flip the three flags together; the texts return with them, wording unchanged.
