## Claude's defaults (shown on the design sheet, his signature covers them)

- The finish step is /sign-up in a "finish" state (`/sign-up?state=finish`). The address shown is
  the signed-in account's own, read from the session, never from the URL. "Not you? Sign out" signs
  out and shows the ordinary sign-in.
- The mail for a new address is the approved "Confirm your email" auth mail (`email-signup-*`), in
  the language of the page. No new mail wording.
- Until the account is finished, opening /account (or arriving from the link or the 6-digit code)
  brings her to the finish step. After Finish account she goes where she was going: her account, or
  back to the checkout she came from.
- "Finished" is decided by the server, not by the browser: an account made after this ships with no
  account-agreement row and no staff role must finish. Sign-up and checkout accounts already have
  their row, so they never see the step. Accounts made before the ship are not asked.
- The tick on the finish step is stored exactly like the /sign-up tick (account_agreement_records,
  surface `sign-up`, choice `create`, text version 2026-09-29).
- The mobile number is saved where /account keeps it today (`user_metadata.phone`). Same check as
  /account: optional, but if present it must hold at least 9 digits.
- Field order on every sign-up form: first name, last name, email, mobile number (optional),
  password (password form only), the tick.
- New strings, four languages, in `app/vamos-i18n-dict.js`: "Finish your account", "Your email is
  confirmed. Add your name to finish your account.", "Your email", "Mobile number (optional)", "So
  your driver can reach you on the day.", "Check the mobile number, including the country code",
  "Finish account", "Not you? Sign out".
