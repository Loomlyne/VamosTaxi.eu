## What later phases inherit if this ships unchanged

**Phase 5 (public surfaces).** Widget switch table is API §7. `same_place` and `place_out_of_box` render the wrong instruction; overnight flights have an `action` and no copy; HMAC failures look like expiry. Home still collects a flight number that the lock will throw away.

**Phase 7 (checkout & payment).** Happy-path coupon or child seat 409s `price_changed` (FC-01). Intent sequence cannot be made PAY-05-safe without a contract change (FC-03). Snapshot write can omit duration and flight (FC-02, FC-10) and blow up on pax 0 (FC-11). Secret rotation mid-checkout is a mass requote disguised as expiry (FC-04).

**Phase 8 (dispatch).** D16 exclusion is either a legal store of Directions seconds or a 30-minute floor that will double-book a Zermatt chauffeur (FC-02). Phone bookings cannot reach the engine without coords and a live Mapbox route (FC-08). No `ops_phone` snapshot write exists (D42).

**Phase 9 (lifecycle).** LIFE-03 refunds a booking-level child-seat line with no allocation rule (FC-05). LIFE-03 100 % refund leaves the coupon burned, or DELETE erases cap evidence (FC-07). LIFE-06 has no `flight_no` on the leg (FC-10). LIFE-07 either sweeps class-change unbound snapshots D42 reintroduced, or never runs and U48 retention is ops_phone-plus-web leftovers (FC-06).

## Non-findings (checked, not silent)

- D11 one-booking / one-snapshot / `price_snapshot_legs` is restated and followed for the web funnel (return = two Directions calls, one class, round-trip discount booking-level). The break is extras `leg_seq` and the missing allocation, not the header shape.
- D7/D8 (embed `basis` + i18n key, never prose) hold in §13; Phase 9 can render lines in four languages **for the chosen class**.
- D19 append-only + two clocks (D47) correctly forbids extending `expires_at`; Phase 7 must not revive the Phase 2 comment.
- D9 / QUOTE-10: no live row ⇒ null totals ⇒ 409 `pricing_not_live` before Stripe. Phone is also blocked until the matrix; that is stated, not a gap.
- U20 is **named** as Phase 7’s. The finding is that API §6’s step order makes the named deferral unimplementable, not that Phase 4 forgot the id.
- Geometry on `QuoteResponse.route.legs[]` is request-scoped and must not be persisted; that is stated. Duration/metres on the lock and on `booking_legs` are the §2.10.1 store.

## Verdict

Phase 4’s engine kernel and five-layer `pricing_live=false` gate are forward-compatible. The **HTTP lock and the snapshot write shape** are not: checkout cannot apply a coupon, Phase 8 cannot legally (or even contractually) fill the duration D16 requires, and Phase 9 cannot refund a return extra or restore a coupon use from what is stored. Harden FC-01 and FC-02 before P2/P7 of the proposed plan split; the rest are additive contract/schema patches in the same phase.
