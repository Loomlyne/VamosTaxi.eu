<objective>
Make surcharge applicability **data**. Today "22:00–06:00" and "pickup is an airport zone" would
have to live in TypeScript, unversioned — a hole straight through Phase 2's D-07, because the rule
a customer was charged under would not be frozen with the version they were charged from.

This plan adds the predicate column, the two `service_zones` columns the predicates need, the
publish gate that refuses an unanswered rule, and the two missing surcharge codes QUOTE-11 and the
U16 shape require.

Purpose: after this plan, every question the engine asks about "does this surcharge apply?" is
answered by a row in a frozen rate version, and an unanswered question blocks publication instead
of being guessed at deploy time.
Output: two additive migrations, one new pgTAP file, an extended publish-gate proof, a regenerated
seed with ten surcharges and typed zones.
</objective>
