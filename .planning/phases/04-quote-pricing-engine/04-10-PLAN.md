<objective>
Everything between an address the customer typed and a pair of metres the kernel can price —
and nothing else.

Three things make this plan more than an HTTP client. First, Mapbox's Product Terms make the
obvious optimisation illegal: the response cache GSD-LAUNCH assumes is barred until an Order
exists, so the cheapest thing to build is the thing we must not build. Second, the two NULLs in
QUOTE-07 behave in opposite directions — a missing polygon must refuse the quote and a missing
minimum advance must wave it through — and getting that backwards either quotes Vladivostok or
blocks a legitimate booking. Third, the owner has no Mapbox account yet (D-47), so every line
here has to be provable with no token, no network and no bill.

Purpose: QUOTE-01's "sees the route drawn" and QUOTE-07's "with a message saying which".
Output: `lib/geo/{mapbox,session,serviceArea}.ts`, the three `/api/geo/*` proxies, and a fixture
set that makes all of it testable offline.
</objective>
