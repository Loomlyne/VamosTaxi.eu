<objective>
Open the anonymous quote identity's READ door, and finish the coupon ledger.

The discovery this plan is built on: `asQuote` resolves to the `anon` Postgres role, and Phase 2
plan 02-08 grants that role SELECT on exactly four content tables and one view. **Nothing in the
pricing schema is readable by the quote path today.** 04-RESEARCH.md's rate-book loading table
assumes a direct `select` — that assumption is wrong against what shipped, and D-34's own words
("`asQuote`/**definer RPCs**") are what this plan implements. Three functions become the grant
surface; the tables stay revoked from every public role, exactly as `settings_public` is the grant
surface for `settings`.

Purpose: QUOTE-03 cannot price without the book, QUOTE-04 cannot mint a deadline without Postgres
authoring it, and QUOTE-06 needs a refusal ladder that speaks in i18n keys rather than SQLSTATEs.
Output: two additive migrations, two new pgTAP files, extended reference-table assertions.
</objective>
