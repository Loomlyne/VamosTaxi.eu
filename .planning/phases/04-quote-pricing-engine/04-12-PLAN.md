<objective>
One lookup, three honest failures, and two columns so Phase 9 has something to shift.

QUOTE-08 reads as the smallest requirement in the phase — "entering a flight number fills in the
landing time" — and it is the one most likely to ship wrong. Three specific ways: taking the first
record of an overnight flight and writing a 00:15 arrival onto yesterday's calendar day; porting
the mock's 45-second poll and quietly becoming the live-tracking product Phase 9 owns; and adding
a private 60-minute buffer because the setting looked empty.

There is also an account that does not exist. ADR-014 §4 defers AeroDataBox explicitly, so the
no-key path is not an edge case here — it is the shipping configuration, and it has to leave the
customer able to type a pickup time.

Purpose: QUOTE-08, and the provenance columns LIFE-06 needs in Phase 9.
Output: one additive migration with its pgTAP proof, `lib/flight/aerodatabox.ts`, and
`GET /api/flight/:no`.
