<objective>
The two public endpoints the whole phase has been building toward, and the ordered list of
refusals between an untrusted body and a signed price.

The reason this is its own plan is that §2's twelve steps are not a suggestion. Step 4 before
step 5 is the difference between a breaker that saves money and one that trips after the bill.
Step 11 before step 12 is the difference between an `exp` authored by Postgres and one authored
by a Worker clock that can drift. A handler that reads top-to-bottom hides that ordering inside
whitespace; an ordered array of named steps makes a reorder a reviewable diff.

The reprice half carries the one rule most likely to be got wrong quietly: a coupon-only reprice
must not re-mint the lock. Re-minting it would reset the 30 minutes on every keystroke in the
coupon field, and QUOTE-04's promise would evaporate without a single test going red.

Purpose: QUOTE-01 through QUOTE-11 stop being modules and become an HTTP surface.
Output: `POST /api/quote`, `POST /api/quote/reprice`, and the step list they share.
</objective>
