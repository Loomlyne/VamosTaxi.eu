<objective>
The pipeline itself: turn a frozen rate book plus a pinned journey into the exact `lines[]` array
that becomes `price_snapshots.lines`, and derive the typed totals from it.

This is where a different order would be different money. D-06 is not a style preference — it is
the reason two surcharges commute, the reason a customer can check a night surcharge against the
fare line above it, and the reason a Stripe dispute packet survives being read by a stranger.

Purpose: QUOTE-03 and QUOTE-05 both reduce to "the array is right and the total is the array".
Output: `lines.ts`, `policy.ts`, `priceQuote.ts` and their unit + property tests. Still no I/O,
still no database, still every amount `null`.
</objective>
