<objective>
The boundary and the vocabulary.

Everything before this plan is arithmetic. This is where untrusted input first meets it, and where
the engine's refusals stop being English sentences in a handler and become a table: one code, one
status, one i18n key, one suggested action, in four languages, in the same commit.

Two rules are doing most of the work. The schema rejects client price inputs **structurally** —
not by ignoring them, which leaves the reviewer wondering, but by failing the parse, which leaves a
log line. And a refused `hourly` is `422 mode_not_offered`, not `400 untrusted_input`, because the
customer asked a reasonable question and deserves a product answer.

Purpose: QUOTE-09's cheapest defence layer, QUOTE-07's "message saying which", and Law 03 for the
whole phase's copy.
Output: `zod` installed and pinned, `schema.ts`, `errors.ts`, and every string in four languages
with `pnpm i18n:check` green.
