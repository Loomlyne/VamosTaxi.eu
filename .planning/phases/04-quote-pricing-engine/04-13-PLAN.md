<objective>
The layer that decides how much a stranger is allowed to cost us, and the one place a signature
is what separates a customer from a bucket.

Three of these rules exist because the obvious version of them is a bypass. An unsigned
`vamos_qs` would let `Cookie: vamos_qs=<fresh uuid>` buy a new 8-per-minute allowance on every
request and reset the Turnstile counter with it — so the cookie is HMAC-signed and an
unverifiable one falls into the *smaller* bucket, not a new one. A breaker that only counts
`/api/quote` would leave `/api/geo/*` — three of the four billable call shapes — uncounted. And a
ceiling denominated in francs would require inventing a Mapbox price nobody has quoted us, so the
sentinel counts units.

The fourth rule is a negative: nothing here builds a Cron that expires quotes. An `expires_at` in
the past with no `booking_id` already is an expired quote; a sweep that rewrites rows to say so
adds a mutation path to an append-only table for no new information.

Purpose: QUOTE-09, at a cost the owner has not yet been billed for.
Output: `lib/abuse/{rate-limit,turnstile,breaker,guards}.ts`, the six routes wired, and Layer 1
recorded as configuration.
</objective>
