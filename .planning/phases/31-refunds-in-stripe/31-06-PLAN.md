<objective>
Build the nightly read-only Stripe-refund reconciliation (D-09, D-12, C-02): the phase's unattended automation agent. It lists Stripe refunds of the last 48 hours, compares them with the site through the plan 31-03 definer, and mails info@ in English only when a refund is missing on its booking or differs, or a short "did not run" mail when Stripe does not answer.

Purpose: refunds now happen in the Stripe Dashboard; a lost webhook would leave a booking without its refund. This check catches it within a day.
Output: English mail renderers + sendRefundCheck, pure core + env wrapper + tests, reader wrapper, alert helpers, cron wiring, local test.
