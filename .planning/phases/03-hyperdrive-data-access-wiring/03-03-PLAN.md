<objective>
Give `apps/web` its data-access surface: two Hyperdrive bindings per named environment, the five
named call-site wrappers with Workers Analytics Engine instrumentation behind them, Placement
Hints pinned at Zurich, and the OpenNext wiring that makes `@vamos/db` importable at all.

No product route is built here (those are Phases 4, 5 and 7). The one route this plan adds is a
dev/staging-gated smoke handler whose only job is to prove `getCloudflareContext()` →
`asAnon` → Hyperdrive works through a real OpenNext build, which D-36 requires and which
nothing else in Phase 3 exercises.

**Precondition (D-30):** Phase 2 is mid-execution. This plan is deliberately database-free at
verification time — everything it asserts is a config grep, a typecheck or an OpenNext build.
The runtime smoke against the local stack belongs to plan 03-06, which owns the Docker stack in
its wave.

**Region (D-25):** the placement target is **`aws:eu-central-2`** (Zurich). `aws:eu-central-1`
appears ~20 times in `03-RESEARCH.md` and is stale everywhere. Never copy it.

**DATA-05's p50 is DEFERRED (D-23/D-27).** This plan lands the instrumentation and the placement;
the number is measured from a deployed staging Worker in plan 03-07. Do not substitute a local
`Date.now()` delta, and do not carry a Frankfurt-derived figure forward as a baseline.

Purpose: every later phase's query work binds to this surface, so getting the binding names,
the role split and the cache posture right here is what keeps Phase 4's pricing reads off a
60-second cache.
Output: a wrangler shape, a typed env, five wrappers, an OpenNext-importable `@vamos/db`, and
the region corrections the research still owes.
</objective>
