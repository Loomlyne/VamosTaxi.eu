## Decisions for the owner's plan signature

Koss signs these with the plan. Each item: what you will see and where, what you lose, the recommended option first (this is what the plans build), one alternative, one example. No amounts are invented: examples name classes and dates only.

1. **The five old test classes right after the update.** Business, Economy, First, Van and mahaha are test data you already decided to remove with your own script.
   - Recommended: the update files them straight into the **Retired classes** list under the class table on **Pricing**, with no reason. They never show as Draft. Your removal script then deletes them for good. You lose nothing.
   - Alternative: the update leaves them alone, and you run the removal script before the update goes live. If the script runs late, Pricing shows five extra Draft rows until it does.
   - Example: the morning after the update, Pricing shows "Retired classes 5" with Business … mahaha. After your script runs, that list is gone.

2. **Retiring a class while a new price book is being prepared.** Today's live price book and older books always keep the class's prices. This choice is only about the **draft price book**, the one you are editing on Pricing and have not published yet.
   - Recommended: Retire also takes the class out of the draft price book. Any prices you typed there for that class and never published are lost. If you bring the class back, its draft row starts empty.
   - Alternative: the draft price book keeps the class's prices, hidden. They do not go public and do not hold up Publish, and they come back with the class.
   - Example: you type new figures for Luxury Van in the draft, then retire Luxury Van, then bring it back a week later. Recommended: the Luxury Van row in the draft is empty. Alternative: your figures are still there.

3. **Paid trips with no chauffeur yet, in a class you retire.** This is fixed by the rules you already signed. A chauffeur whose class is retired cannot be given a trip, and Assign only offers chauffeurs of the trip's own class. So moving a chauffeur to another class does not help (the picture spec said it would; that was wrong). Trips that already have a chauffeur keep him. The only way to assign such a trip is to bring the class back, assign, then retire it again. The assignment stays.
   - Recommended: the Retire dialog on **Pricing** shows one more grey line under the chauffeur line, so you can assign those trips before you retire. Same look as the signed chauffeur line (sheet 6). Proposed wording: "2 paid trips ahead have no chauffeur yet" / "1 paid trip ahead has no chauffeur yet"; German "2 kommende bezahlte Fahrten haben noch keinen Chauffeur" / "1 kommende bezahlte Fahrt hat noch keinen Chauffeur"; French "2 courses payées à venir n’ont pas encore de chauffeur" / "1 course payée à venir n’a pas encore de chauffeur"; Arabic with the signed count forms: 1 "رحلة واحدة مدفوعة قادمة بلا سائق بعد", 2 "رحلتان مدفوعتان قادمتان بلا سائق بعد", 5 "5 رحلات مدفوعة قادمة بلا سائق بعد", 11 "11 رحلةً مدفوعةً قادمةً بلا سائق بعد", 100 "100 رحلة مدفوعة قادمة بلا سائق بعد". The hand-over also tells you: bring the class back to assign them.
   - Alternative: no line in the dialog. Only the hand-over says how to assign such trips.
   - Example: Economy Van has one paid trip on 20 October with nobody assigned. You click Retire on Economy Van and read "1 paid trip ahead has no chauffeur yet". You cancel, assign the trip on its booking page, then retire.

4. **A class you bring back, and its old price.** You signed on 11 October that "a class brought back needs a new price before going live". The signed Bring back message says the same: "Add a price in the price book, then go live".
   - Recommended: after Bring back, Go live shows "Price in the live price book" as missing until you publish a price book that has a price for that class. The price still sitting in today's live price book from before the retire does not count. If the draft you publish still holds the old figures, that counts, because you chose them by publishing.
   - Alternative: the old price counts. Go live works straight away, and customers see the price the live book had when you retired the class. This would change your 11 October decision.
   - Example: Luxury Van is retired in March and brought back in May. Recommended: you check its price in the draft, publish, then Go live. Alternative: Go live works in May at March's price.

5. **A class waiting as a Draft must not hold up a price change.** Today, a class with an empty price row in the draft price book stops Publish for every class.
   - Recommended: when you add a class or bring one back, its empty row in the draft price book is parked. Publish works for all other classes while it has no price. To give it a price, type its start fare and per-km price on Pricing and save. Nothing else to tick. After the next Publish, Go live works (with the photo in place).
   - Alternative: keep today's rule. An empty row blocks Publish of the whole draft price book until you price the class or retire it.
   - Example: you add "Business" as a Draft on Monday and want to raise the Economy Sedan per-km price on Tuesday. Recommended: Publish works. Alternative: Publish refuses until Business has a price.

6. **Seats and Bags in the Go live list.**
   - Recommended: as today, every class always has seats and bags. Add class fills in 3 and 3 and you change them. So these two lines always show as done, and only Photo and Price can be missing.
   - Alternative: a new class starts with seats and bags empty, and Go live waits until you fill them. This is new behaviour that is not on the signed pictures, so it needs a new picture first.
   - Example: you add "Business" and do not touch seats. Recommended: Seats reads 3, done. Alternative: Go live lists "Seats" as missing.

7. **The chauffeur editor when his class was retired (Chauffeurs page).**
   - Recommended: the Class list shows Live classes only. If you edit something else (phone, plate), he keeps his old class, and the red line "Retired class: pick a new one" stays until you pick a Live class.
   - Alternative: the Class list also shows his current class greyed out and marked Retired. That adds one new line in four languages to sign.
   - Example: Ahmed is on Economy Van, now retired, and you change his phone number. Recommended: he stays on Economy Van with the red line, and the list offers only Live classes.

8. **Unpaid bookings in a class you retire.** While the class is retired, these are refused with the messages that already exist: new quotes, the new-trip form, changing a booking into that class, and changing date or address on a booking in that class. Paid trips keep their chauffeur. A pay link you already sent is different: it keeps its price until it runs out (24 hours after you sent it).
   - Recommended: leave the pay link as it is. The customer can still pay what you offered. If you do not want to run that trip, cancel the booking. A test pins all of this so it cannot change by accident.
   - Alternative: a pay link stops working once its class is retired, and the customer reads that the link has expired. This needs a new plan that changes checkout, plus a customer message you sign.
   - Example: Monday you send a pay link for an Economy Van trip. Tuesday you retire Economy Van. Wednesday the customer pays. Recommended: the payment goes through, and the trip waits for a chauffeur (see decision 3). Alternative: the customer is told the link has expired.

9. **What counts as "a price" for Go live.** A price row you set to "Hidden from public" never counts, because customers cannot book it.
   - Recommended: Go live needs a start fare and per-km price for the class in the live price book.
   - Alternative: a fixed-route price for the class (for example Zurich Airport to Laax) also counts. The class then only shows on its fixed routes.
   - Example: you add "Ski shuttle", priced only on the airport-to-Laax route. Recommended: Go live says the price is missing. Alternative: Go live works.
