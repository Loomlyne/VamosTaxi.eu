## Decisions, signed by Koss 2026-10-12

Koss answered every item on 2026-10-12 (about 19:30 +04, relayed by the controller). Each item: what you will see and where, the option you chose (this is what the plans build), the option not chosen, one example. No amounts are invented: examples name classes and dates only.

1. **The five old test classes.** Business, Economy, First, Van and mahaha are test data you remove with your own script.
   - **Signed 2026-10-12: you run your removal script first, before the update.** The update itself changes no class. It only checks that these five are gone: if any of them is still there, the update stops and changes nothing, and the controller tells you. The order is: (a) the controller saves the five classes' rows under an archive tag on GitHub and a different model checks your script, then you run it; (b) the controller checks, read only, that the five are gone and that no class other than your three live classes is waiting as a Draft, and stops if not; (c) the update to the database; (d) the new site version.
   - Not chosen: the update files them under Retired classes and your script removes them later.
   - Example: Monday morning you run the script; the controller's check shows only Economy Sedan, Economy Van and Luxury Van; then the update goes in. Pricing never shows the five old classes again.

2. **Retiring a class while a new price book is being prepared.** Today's live price book and older books always keep the class's prices. This choice is only about the **draft price book**, the one you are editing on Pricing and have not published yet.
   - **Signed 2026-10-12: Recommended.** Retire also takes the class out of the draft price book. Any prices you typed there for that class and never published are lost. If you bring the class back, its draft row starts empty.
   - Not chosen: the draft price book keeps the class's prices, hidden. They do not go public and do not hold up Publish, and they come back with the class.
   - Example: you type new figures for Luxury Van in the draft, then retire Luxury Van, then bring it back a week later. Signed: the Luxury Van row in the draft is empty.

3. **Paid trips with no chauffeur yet, in a class you retire.** This is fixed by the rules you already signed. A chauffeur whose class is retired cannot be given a trip, and Assign only offers chauffeurs of the trip's own class. So moving a chauffeur to another class does not help (the picture spec said it would; that was wrong). Trips that already have a chauffeur keep him. The only way to assign such a trip is to bring the class back, assign, then retire it again. The assignment stays.
   - **Signed 2026-10-12: Recommended** (the exact wording is signed with picture sheet 11). The Retire dialog on **Pricing** shows one more grey line under the chauffeur line, so you can assign those trips before you retire. Same look as the signed chauffeur line (sheet 6). Proposed wording: "2 paid trips ahead have no chauffeur yet" / "1 paid trip ahead has no chauffeur yet"; German "2 kommende bezahlte Fahrten haben noch keinen Chauffeur" / "1 kommende bezahlte Fahrt hat noch keinen Chauffeur"; French "2 courses payées à venir n’ont pas encore de chauffeur" / "1 course payée à venir n’a pas encore de chauffeur"; Arabic with the signed count forms: 1 "رحلة واحدة مدفوعة قادمة بلا سائق بعد", 2 "رحلتان مدفوعتان قادمتان بلا سائق بعد", 5 "5 رحلات مدفوعة قادمة بلا سائق بعد", 11 "11 رحلةً مدفوعةً قادمةً بلا سائق بعد", 100 "100 رحلة مدفوعة قادمة بلا سائق بعد". The hand-over also tells you: bring the class back to assign them.
   - Not chosen: no line in the dialog. Only the hand-over says how to assign such trips.
   - Example: Economy Van has one paid trip on 20 October with nobody assigned. You click Retire on Economy Van and read "1 paid trip ahead has no chauffeur yet". You cancel, assign the trip on its booking page, then retire.

4. **A class you bring back, and its old price.** You signed on 11 October that "a class brought back needs a new price before going live". The signed Bring back message says the same: "Add a price in the price book, then go live".
   - **Signed 2026-10-12: Recommended.** After Bring back, Go live shows "Price in the live price book" as missing until you publish a price book that has a price for that class. The price still sitting in today's live price book from before the retire does not count. If the draft you publish still holds the old figures, that counts, because you chose them by publishing.
   - Not chosen: the old price counts. Go live works straight away, and customers see the price the live book had when you retired the class. This would change your 11 October decision.
   - Example: Luxury Van is retired in March and brought back in May. Signed: you check its price in the draft, publish, then Go live.

5. **A class waiting as a Draft must not hold up a price change.** Today, a class with an empty price row in the draft price book stops Publish for every class.
   - **Signed 2026-10-12: Recommended.** When you add a class or bring one back, its empty row in the draft price book is parked. Publish works for all other classes while it has no price. To give it a price, type its start fare, per-km price, seats and bags on Pricing and save (seats and bags because of decision 6). Nothing else to tick. After the next Publish, Go live works (with the photo in place).
   - Not chosen: keep today's rule. An empty row blocks Publish of the whole draft price book until you price the class or retire it.
   - Example: you add "Business" as a Draft on Monday and want to raise the Economy Sedan per-km price on Tuesday. Signed: Publish works.
