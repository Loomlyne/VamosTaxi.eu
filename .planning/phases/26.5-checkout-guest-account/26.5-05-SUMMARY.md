# Phase 26.5 Plan 05: Account provisioning after payment Summary

After a first successful settle (and after the confirmation mail), the queue consumer creates an unconfirmed, credential-less auth user from the PAY-time request and sends one "finish your account" mail with a magic link. Consent is not touched.

## Commits
- f6a18da1 feat(26.5-05): account_ready and account_signin mails in four languages, hook switch
- e19cdfa5 feat(26.5-05): provisionCheckoutAccount with definer reads and per-address limiter
- f7dca921 feat(26.5-05): provision the checkout account after the confirmation mail on a first settle

## What it does
- `account_ready` (link only) and `account_signin` (link + code) in en/de/fr/ar (Swiss German "ss"). The email hook renders `account_signin` for `signup` when `vamos_account_origin` starts with `checkout`; other signups and magic-link mails are unchanged.
- `readCheckoutAccountRequest` / `readCheckoutAccountUserState` in system-reads.ts, each one definer function called as vamos_system (guard test green).
- `provisionCheckoutAccount(row, deps)`: skipped / limited / failed / exists / created, never throws. Limiter key `account-mail:<lowercased email>` on `AUTH_RATE_LIMITER` (missing binding = refuse). Only `checkoutAuthAdmin` is used. `email_exists` + unconfirmed + checkout-origin re-sends the mail; other existing users get nothing. Events carry the booking id and a reason only.
- Link: `https://vamostaxi.site/api/auth/callback?token_hash=...&type=<verification_type ?? magiclink>&next=/<locale>/account`.
- settle.ts: called in its own try/catch right after the confirmation try/catch, only in the first-settle non-extra branch; a rejection or `"failed"` emits `account_provision_failed` with `bookingId` and still acks.
