# Phase 26.5 Plan 06: Account choice on /checkout Summary

The account choice (guest, sign in, create) sits at the top of section 2 on `/checkout`, sign-in works in place, PAY enforces it, and the four-width visual spec passes in en, de and ar.

## What changed

- `layout.tsx` and `CheckoutSettings.tsx`: `guestAccountsOn` and `accountCreateAvailable` come from the server as booleans (false on any error). `CheckoutPage.tsx` was not touched and imports nothing from `lib/supabase` (the recipe's correction: the settings are filled in the layout).
- `CheckoutForm.tsx`: account state and setters on the context; `firstSectionError` with an `account` state; `accountIntentBlock` builds `body.account` (return_to = this page's path and query); `mapAccountCode` handles `sign_in_first` (sent stage, choice flips to Sign in, heading focused), `account_consent_required`, `account_check_failed`, `account_create_unavailable`, `rate_limited` (`payRateLimited`) and `pay_limit` (`payLimit`). `/api/checkout/me` is now `refreshSignedIn`, called on mount, after the code sign-in and on window focus; it fills only empty fields, announces "Signed in as", and moves focus to the first empty required field or PAY. Changing the e-mail (or a resume answer replacing it) resets the Create tick. The 26.4.2 flight-edit challenge and the PAY re-sign path are untouched.
- `ContactSection.tsx`: `AccountChoice` + `CheckoutSignIn` before the contact fields, an invisible `TurnstileWidget action="account"` only when a check can run, a hairline divider. The header sign-in link is gone while the panel shows; "Signed in as" stays when signed in. Contact fields are removed (not hidden) while Sign in is chosen and nobody is signed in.
- `checkout.css`: `[data-co-account]` wrapper, divider, and a rule so the invisible account check reserves no space (the shared Turnstile host reserves 65 px). `account-choice.css`: links in the notice read in charcoal (the brand link underline is yellow; the panel carries no yellow).
- `phase-26-5-laws.test.ts`: the 13 `it.todo` are real assertions (39 tests).
- `checkout-account.spec.ts`: live, 54 tests plus 6 screenshot tests (only with `SHOT_DIR`). Screenshots in `screens/`: en 1440, 1024, 768, 390, de 390, ar 390.
