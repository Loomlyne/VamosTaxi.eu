<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

- **D-01 (owner):** The choice sits at the **top of section 2 "Who is travelling"** on `/checkout`, before the contact fields: Continue as guest · Sign in · Create an account. A signed-in customer skips it and gets name/e-mail/phone prefilled. Same page; no extra step after PAY.
- **D-02 (owner, completing his sentence):** A guest automatically gets an **account created from their e-mail, with no password**. They sign in later with a **magic link** to that e-mail.
- **D-03 (owner, direction):** For now passwords and one-time codes stay. **Later** (separate job, not 26.5) all passwords go: sign-in / create account will offer only magic link or passkey, always with an e-mail. 26.5 must not add anything that depends on passwords.
- **D-04 (owner):** If the guest's e-mail **already has an account**, checkout **asks them to sign in first** (a sign-in link is sent) before PAY. Security note for UI-SPEC/plan: this reveals that an e-mail is registered (account enumeration) — keep the wording neutral (e.g. "Check your inbox — we sent a sign-in link") and rate-limit/Turnstile the check; behaviour stays as the owner chose.
- **D-05 (owner):** An **unconfirmed e-mail sees nothing** beyond what exists today: the booking is reachable only through the confirmation e-mail's manage link; the account shows bookings only after one magic-link sign-in (e-mail confirmed). No one can see another person's bookings by typing their e-mail. (Consistent with quick 260929-acl: linking happens only for a confirmed e-mail.)
- **D-06 (rule, control session):** Creating an account for someone who did not ask needs **consent wording**. That is legal copy — never invented. The panel shows a `data-tok` gap ("account creation notice") until the owner supplies the text in en/de/fr/ar. The customer's consent is **logged server-side** (consent_log or equivalent), not only in the browser.
- **D-07:** Sign-in from checkout returns to the same checkout with the trip, class and extras kept (existing `returnTo` + sessionStorage-for-this-tab behaviour from 26.3 / quick G1).
- **D-08:** Four languages, Arabic RTL, Vamos design system only, no glow, no tinted yellow, 1440/1024/768/390, 44/54 px — as every surface.

- **D-09 (owner, 2026-09-29):** While the guest-account switch is OFF (notice text not yet supplied), "Continue as guest" is exactly today's guest checkout: no tick box, no account, nothing recorded, and the known-e-mail sign-in-first rule (D-04) applies to guests only once the switch is on. No fake control.
- **D-10 (owner, 2026-09-29, corrected same day):** "Create an account" works while the notice text is not yet supplied: the account is created after payment and a link finishes it. **No consent is recorded** (no checkout_account_consent row, no signup_consent / consent_log row) until the owner's four notice texts exist — a record would name a text the customer never saw. From then on consent is recorded with the notice version. (Earlier wording "recorded when she confirms" was wrong and is withdrawn.)
