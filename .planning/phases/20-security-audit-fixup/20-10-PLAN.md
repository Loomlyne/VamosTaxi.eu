# 20-10 — Refunds by hand (finding F11)

**Owner decision, question form, 2026-09-30:** "You refund by hand." A customer's cancel goes
through; the booking shows "Refund due" on the dashboard; the admin presses Refund. Nothing is
sent to Stripe without his click.

## What exists today (read at main e27014c1, not changed)

- Cancel **more than 24 hours** before pickup: the site refunds 100 % by itself
  (`apps/web/lib/lifecycle/paid-cancel.ts`). With a real Stripe key (`sk_live_`) it refuses and marks
  the booking "refund failed" (line 115-117).
- Cancel **inside 24 hours**: already by hand. The booking is cancelled at once, the refund waits
  for the team ("Refund under review"), the admin decides on the dashboard.
- So the hand path, its state, its dashboard button and its mails exist. This plan moves the
  more-than-24-hours case onto that same path with 100 % pre-filled, and removes the automatic call.
