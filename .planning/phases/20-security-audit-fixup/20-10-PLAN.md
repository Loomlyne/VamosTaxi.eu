# 20-10 — Refunds by hand (finding F11)

**Owner decision, question form, 2026-09-30:** "You refund by hand." A customer's cancel goes
through; the booking shows "Refund due" on the dashboard; the admin presses Refund. Nothing is
sent to Stripe without his click.

## What exists today (read at main e27014c1, not changed)

- Cancel **more than 24 hours** before pickup: the site refunds 100 % by itself
  (`apps/web/lib/lifecycle/paid-cancel.ts`). With a real Stripe key (`sk_live_`) it refuses and marks
  the booking "refund failed" (line 115-117).
- Cancel **inside 24 hours**: already by hand. The booking is cancelled at once, the refund waits
  for the team ("Refund under review"), the admin decides on the dashboard.
- So the hand path, its state, its dashboard button and its mails exist. This plan moves the
  more-than-24-hours case onto that same path with 100 % pre-filled, and removes the automatic call.

## Added 2026-09-30: refund across both payments (26.2 row B6, owner answer "Refund across both payments")

Found by the 26.2 audit (`gsd/phase-26.2-audit-2`, `975c3ab4`, `26.2-BP-B.md` line 13), not yet built.

- Today `ops_refund_record` records against one payment and caps at what is left of it
  (migration `20260928150000`); `lib/ops/refund.ts:88-104,122` uses the first payment only.
- Example: Mia paid CHF 100 for the trip and later CHF 20 extra fare. "Refund 100 %" asks for 120,
  the answer is `refund-exceeds-remaining`, nothing is refunded. The extra fare can never be
  refunded from the dashboard.
- Also today: Stripe is called before the record is written. If the record fails, the dashboard
  says "Refund failed" although the money left; Retry completes it.

Tasks added to this plan:

7. The admin Refund covers every captured payment of the booking. One Stripe refund per payment,
   each recorded against its own payment, in the order the owner picks below.
8. Record first, then Stripe, then mark the record sent: a failure between the two can no longer
   show "failed" for money that left. Each step is safe to repeat (idempotency key per payment).
9. Tests: 100 + 20 refunded 100 % makes two refunds of 100 and 20; a partial refund follows the
   chosen order; a failing second call leaves the first recorded and the rest shown as still due.

Owner decisions, question form, 2026-09-30:

- **Which payment is refunded:** his words: "i get to choose which based on the booking if both
  payment on the same booking i get to choose". So when a booking has more than one captured
  payment, the dashboard Refund shows each payment (what it was for, amount, already refunded,
  left) and the admin picks the payment and the amount. One payment = today's form, no picker.
  "Refund 100 %" of the whole booking refunds every payment in full.
- **Second Stripe call fails:** show what went and what is left ("CHF 100 refunded, CHF 20 still
  due") with Retry for the rest. Nothing hidden, nothing sent twice.
- **Settle re-check of amount and currency (F14):** leave it. Accepted: the price is fixed by the
  server when the payment page is created and the trigger refuses a charge that differs.

## Design note for the payment picker — SIGNED by the owner, question form, 2026-09-30

Dashboard booking screen, inside the existing refund block (`app/ops/OpsDetail.dc.html`,
`[data-ops-refund-region]`). Design-system parts only (List rows with a radio, the % Input, the
Confirm and Decline buttons, the danger Alert). en, de, fr, ar. Checked at 1440, 1024, 768, 390.
A booking with one captured payment looks exactly as today (no list).

```
REFUND DUE
Cancelled more than 24 h before pickup.

Which payment?
(o) All payments              CHF 120.00
( ) Trip        30 Sep 14:02  CHF 100.00
      refunded CHF 0.00 - left CHF 100.00
( ) Extra fare  30 Sep 16:40  CHF  20.00
      refunded CHF 0.00 - left CHF  20.00

[ 100 % ]   [ CONFIRM REFUND ]  [ DECLINE REFUND ]

--- after one of two refunds failed ---

! CHF 100.00 refunded. CHF 20.00 still due.
  Stripe did not accept the second refund.
[ TRY AGAIN ]
```

The percentage applies to the chosen payment, or to every payment when "All payments" is chosen.
Amounts come from the database (`VamosLocale.money`), never typed by the admin.
