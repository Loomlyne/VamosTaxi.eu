Exceptions (already shipped — do not retune, do not copy onto a new bar):

New rules use logical properties only (`margin-block-end`, `padding-inline-start`). No `margin-left`, `margin-right`, `padding-left`, `padding-right`.

## Typography

Three sizes, two weights. Do not invent a fourth size. Do not set a size on `PendingSlot`. The wrapper sets size only where inheritance would be wrong.

| Role | Size | Token | Weight | Line height | Usage |
|------|------|-------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Body | 16px | `--vt-body-md` | 400 | 1.6 (`--vt-body-leading`) | Privacy slot. Inherits `[data-lg-prose]` (`LegalPage.css:14-18`). Do not set a size there. |
| Label | 14px | `--vt-body-sm` | 400 | 1.6 | Banner slot and cookies row. Matches `.vt-ck-body` and `.vt-table`. Set on the wrapper so the slot does not inherit the 20px title or the 16px page body. |
| Heading | 20px | `--vt-heading-3` | 600 | 1.18 | Existing `.vt-ck-title` only. Do not apply it to the slot. |

Weights: 400 on the blank name, 600 only where it already exists (banner title, and the `::after` " TBC"). Do not set 500 or 700 on the slot.

**Live chrome, not a declared size:** `[data-tok]::after` is 11px, weight 600, tracking `var(--vt-label-tracking)`, uppercase (`laws.css:65`). Leave it. Do not bump it to 12px. Do not put "TBC" in the label prop — the stylesheet already appends it.

The blank name does not truncate. No `max-inline-size`, no `overflow: hidden`, no `text-overflow`, no fixed `inline-size` or `block-size`. The pill keeps `white-space: nowrap` from `laws.css:64`. That overflows rather than clips. The prescribed names fit the banner content width (sheet padding 24px, inset 16px). `text-wrap: balance` and `pretty` do not apply to a nowrap pill. Do not set them.

`font-family` on the banner wrapper: `var(--vt-font-body)`. Privacy inherits Poppins from prose. Do not use Qurova on the slot.

They do not fit the banner content box (452px sheet minus 48px padding at `CookieBanner.css:86-92`, or viewport minus 32px inset and 48px padding below that). ## Accessibility

- The slot is a `span` inside a `div`. Not a `button`. Not an `a`. Not `role="button"`. Not `role="status"`. Not `role="alert"`.
- Do not add `aria-label`, `aria-describedby`, `aria-expanded`, or `aria-pressed`. The visible blank name is the name. Extra ARIA that implies a control is a fail.
- Do not add `onClick`. Do not link it to `/cookies`. The banner already has that link (`CookieBanner.tsx:180-182`).
- Not in the tab order. Do not set `tabindex`.
- Hit area does not apply. Do not inflate the pill to 24px or 44px.
- Four locales ship together. Arabic `dir="rtl"` is already set on the document. Do not set `dir` on the slot. Do not add `.vt-dir-keep`. Other English pills already render in Arabic without it.
- Text stays selectable. No `user-select: none`.
- The existing `title` on `PendingSlot` stays. It is a tooltip, not a control, and not a Meta sentence.
