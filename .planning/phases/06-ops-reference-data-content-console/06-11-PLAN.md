<objective>
Give staff the business-settings editor OPS-09 asks for, and put the versioned policy beside it as
something they can read but not touch.

`OpsSettings.dc.html` has six panes: company, policy, locale, payments, notify, security. Two of
them do not survive contact with the applied schema, and both corrections are the point of this plan.

**The policy pane is not editable.** The mock offers `minAdvance`, `cancelWindow`, `airportWait` and
`cityWait` as fields to type into. Phase 2 split those values out of `settings` into
`settings_versions` precisely so they could not be typed into: the table is append-only (the
`…19_append_only.sql` trigger, the revoke, and FORCE RLS), `vamos_staff` holds `select, insert` and
nothing else, and `settings_versions_admin_write` is `AS RESTRICTIVE FOR INSERT` on `app.is_admin()`.
An UPDATE raises. This is deliberate: `price_snapshots.settings_version_id` is `on delete restrict`
so a sold booking's policy provenance cannot move, and ADR-005 makes the public cancellation copy a
read of the same row. Editing the number in place would rewrite what past customers were sold under.
So the pane becomes a read-only current-version display (D-27), and the module this plan writes
exports no mutation against that table at all — a structural guarantee, not a discipline.

**The security pane is not business settings.** The mock's `secEmail`, `pw1`/`pw2`, `passkeyAdded`
and `deleteOpen` are the signed-in staff member's own account, not the company's configuration. They
belong on the profile screen (06-17), and two of them contradict the project besides: staff use TOTP,
not passkeys, and a staff account is deactivated by an admin, never self-deleted (`staff_admin_write`).
This plan ships the five genuinely-editable panes and leaves account security to 06-17.

Purpose: the company's own details, defaults and toggles are editable without a deploy; the policy a
booking is sold under is legible and immovable.
Output: five editable panes, one read-only policy card, one action, one spec.
</objective>

  company, address, uid_number, phone, email        text not null default ''
  default_lang     text not null default 'en' check in ('en','de','fr','ar')

`public.settings_versions` — append-only policy history:
  id bigint identity, slug text unique, label text, effective_from timestamptz, created_by uuid
  free_cancel_hours, modification_deadline_hours, min_advance_minutes            integer, nullable
  airport_waiting_minutes, city_waiting_minutes                                  integer, nullable
  manage_link_validity_days, quote_lock_minutes, checkout_window_minutes         integer, nullable
  round_trip_discount_percent numeric(5,2) nullable
  night_window_start / night_window_end time nullable, night_window_tz text default 'Europe/Zurich'
  cancellation_tiers jsonb not null default '[]', CHECK jsonb_typeof = 'array'
  policy_doc_slug, policy_doc_version text
  The current version is the newest `effective_from <= now()`.
  Tier shape: [{"from_hours_before":24,"refund_percent":100}, …, {"no_show":true,"refund_percent":0}]

RLS:
  `settings` — in the seventeen-table staff working set; full CRUD to `vamos_staff` behind
    `settings_staff_gate` (RESTRICTIVE, `app.is_staff()`). Not admin-only.
  `settings_versions` — deliberately OUT of that loop (F-02). `grant select, insert` only, plus
    `settings_versions_staff_gate` (RESTRICTIVE FOR ALL) and `settings_versions_admin_write`
    (RESTRICTIVE **FOR INSERT**, `app.is_admin()`). UPDATE/DELETE are revoked and the append-only
    trigger raises regardless. 06-01's pgTAP file proves both halves.

Public path (not read by this screen, listed so it is not duplicated): `public.settings_public` is a
definer view over `settings` publishing phone, email, default_lang, default_currency, accepts_card,
accepts_twint, accepts_cash to `vamos_public`/`anon`/`authenticated`. Editing those six fields here
changes the public footer and contact page.

Audit: `settings` and `settings_versions` are both in `tg_audit_row`'s attach list. No manual audit
write. **No touch trigger exists** — `updated_at` is set by the statement.

From 06-02: `requireStaffClaims()`. From 06-07: `mapSqlState`. From 06-01: the pgTAP contract proving
a console-connection UPDATE on `settings_versions` raises.
