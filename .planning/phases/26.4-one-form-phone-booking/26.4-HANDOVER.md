## What 26.4 delivers

Plans 26.4-01…10. Decisions D-01…D-16 in `26.4-CONTEXT.md`.

- **Phone and tablet (≤1080 px):** the hero box is replaced by one bar ("Where to?"). A tap opens a full-screen sheet with four steps (From, To, When, Travellers), a "1 of 4" mark, Back, and a close button that keeps the entries for the tab only. One button per step, never disabled; an incomplete step shows a warning that names what is missing. SEE PRICES goes to `/checkout` with the trip in the URL. The bar docks under the header on scroll. Reload starts empty; nothing is stored. Laptop (≥1081) keeps the 26.3 box. A laptop-bar follow-up is coming separately.
- **One form:** no trip types. Service links (airport transfers, city to city, hourly, …) open the same booking; `?service=` no longer changes the form; the old mode leftovers are removed.
- **Airport fee (D-08):** added only when the pickup address is an airport. A flight number alone adds nothing. Amounts are unchanged; only the trigger changed.
- **Flight number (D-09):** required only for an airport pickup, optional otherwise, on the home box, the sheet, checkout Edit trip, dashboard New trip and at PAY. A flight-only change on checkout re-signs the lock and keeps the price.
- **Fare parts (D-10, D-12):** start + per km + airport pickup fee + at most one city or canton pair + extras, then VAT, then voucher. Proven with fixture price-book rows (plain, airport, pair, everything together). Old quote locks with `fare_kind` still verify.
- **Stripe product name (D-14):** "Vamos Taxi transfer" instead of "Airport transfer" for every ride.
- **Dashboard pricing sentence (D-16):** "One row covers A to B and B to A", in four languages. The dead city-price save path is gone.
- **D-15** (New trip: live extras, flight never re-quotes, Save accepted) came from quick 260929-nts and is green here.
