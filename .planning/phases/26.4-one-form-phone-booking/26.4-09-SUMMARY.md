# Phase 26.4 Plan 09: Home bar and sheet wiring Summary

Home at 1080 px and under shows one BookingBar (plus a docked twin under the floating header on scroll) and the full-screen BookingSheet, both fed by the laptop box's single trip state; from 1081 px the 26.3 box is unchanged.

## Commits
- 269b1602: bar + sheet mounted, `checkTrip()`/`goCheckout()` shared, old shell CSS removed, one 1080/1081 boundary, dock (IntersectionObserver), `/#book` interceptor, dest-pick, resize, bfcache reset, unit pins
- 7083a07f: end-to-end spec; dock offset fix, chips keep their own handler, Lenis anchor scroll stopped on `/#book`, `html{scroll-behavior:smooth}` removed
- box spec: skipped at <=1080 (comment "26.4 D-01")

## What changed
- `checkTrip()` (pure, the 26.3 rules plus D-09) and `goCheckout()` (same URLSearchParams, `location.assign`); the box button and the sheet's `onSubmit` both use them. A failing sheet submit reopens at the first failing step.
- `NARROW()` replaces the 699/1079 tiers. `id="book"` sits on the bar wrapper at <=1080 and on the box card at >=1081; the box is `display:none` at <=1080.
- Dock: `IntersectionObserver` on the in-hero bar with top margin = header bottom; docked bar `position:fixed`, `z-index:190`, 200ms in / 140ms out, hidden while the sheet or the header menu is open (MutationObserver on `data-hd-open`). Placement uses the header height plus its float offset, not its live rect (the header slides).
- `/#book` links on home at <=1080 open the sheet at step 1; if the header menu is open it is closed first (its own body-lock release must not clobber the sheet's). Arriving on `/#book` lands on the bar. HowItWorks chips fill To and open the sheet (`detail.handled = true`). At >=1081 links scroll and focus From.
- Resize past 1080 closes the sheet silently; the box shows the same values. `pageshow` with `persisted` resets the trip. The sheet itself sets `inert` on the page behind, locks scroll, stops Lenis and restores scroll (asserted in the spec).
- No dictionary edits were needed: every string was already in `app/vamos-i18n-dict.js` (plans 04, 05, 06); plan 08 reported none missing.

## Deviations
1. [Rule 1] HowItWorks chips: the plan said "set To (as today)", but the existing handler filled the pickup. Changed to To, as UI-SPEC S4 requires (also at >=1081).
2. [Rule 1] Removed `html{scroll-behavior:smooth}` from home (pre-existing, conflicts with the Lenis rule; plan acceptance requires none).
3. [Rule 3] `/#book` interceptor calls `stopPropagation` (Lenis's anchor listener does not honour `preventDefault`) and skips HowItWorks chips (they own their click and dispatch `vamos:dest-pick`).
4. [Rule 3] Header menu open + `/#book` click: the menu is closed via its own button and the sheet opens after `data-hd-open` clears (else the header's body-unlock leaves the page unlocked or stuck).
5. `inert` is applied by BookingSheet itself (plan 05), not duplicated in home; asserted in the e2e spec.
6. `home-widget.spec.ts` was not edited: it has no 1079/shell dependence in its functional tests. Its `screenshot * populated widget` tests fail at every width including 1440 (dev server on :4292 not ready / old widget seeding), unrelated to this plan and not rebaselined.
7. Known limit: if the host's `checkTrip()` fails after the sheet already flushed its history entries (a race with the 3-hour lead time), the sheet stays open at the failing step without history entries.
