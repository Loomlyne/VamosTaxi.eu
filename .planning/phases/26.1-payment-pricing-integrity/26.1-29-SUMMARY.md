# Phase 26.1 Plan 29: Traveller honours the pay-link hold Summary

The traveller's own checkout and requote now share the 24-hour pay-link clock: intent accepts an expired lock while `bookings.hold_until` is open (signature still verified), and requote leaves a held booking and its Stripe session alone.

## Accomplishments

- `checkIntentAgainstLock` gets `holdUntilIso`; `effectiveExpiry(exp, hold)` = later of the two, used for both the Worker and Postgres expiry steps. HMAC check runs first regardless.
- `runCheckoutIntent` passes `deps.holdUntilIso` through; the intent route loads it via `select public.checkout_booking_hold_until($quote_id)` under `asCheckout(env, null, …)` before `runCheckoutIntent`.
- Requote route loads the hold first (after CSRF, before the session lookup). Open hold → `409 { ok:false, code:"hold_open", hold_until }`; no expire, no cancel. No hold / past hold → byte-for-byte previous flow.
