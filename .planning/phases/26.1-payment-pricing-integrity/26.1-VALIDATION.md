## INT-06 race (D-22) — why no Playwright race test

A true two-payer race needs two browsers completing Stripe Elements against the sandbox at the same instant; Playwright cannot drive Stripe's hosted iframes deterministically in CI, and a flaky race test would be worse than none. The race is covered at each layer instead: pgTAP proves a second succeeded charge becomes `duplicate` with no 23505 (26.1-02); vitest proves the consumer refunds it by PaymentIntent with an idempotency key and expires other open sessions (26.1-05); pgTAP + vitest prove the recipient sees `pay_link_refunded_duplicate` (26.1-15/16); 26.1-28 checks the live sandbox for any `duplicate` row without a refund.
