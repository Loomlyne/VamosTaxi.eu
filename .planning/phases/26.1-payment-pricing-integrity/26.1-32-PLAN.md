<objective>
Close VERIFICATION.md gap 1 (BLOCKER): "Coupon cap can be skipped at payment." Success criterion 3, requirement INT-04, decision D-11.

`runCheckoutIntent` (`apps/web/lib/checkout/intent.ts`) currently derives the coupon to re-evaluate at payment from `body.coupon` only. The signed lock's class total (`payload.class_totals`, read via the `reprice` board) is already post-coupon from quote time — `payload.coupon` carries which coupon that is. A request that sends `coupon: null` while replaying a lock signed with a coupon is charged the discounted total, `evaluate_coupon` is never called, no `coupon_redemptions` row is written, and the per-user/global cap is never checked. `/api/checkout/pay-link/route.ts` shares `runCheckoutIntent`, so it inherits the same exposure for booking creation, plus its own smaller display-only exposure (the confirmation email's coupon label reads `body.coupon` instead of the lock's coupon).

Purpose: D-11 ("Coupon caps are enforced at payment") must hold for every request shape, not only the one the legitimate client happens to send.
Output: the coupon actually charged is always the coupon the verified lock priced, always re-evaluated with the payer's identity, with a closed refusal ladder when body and lock disagree.
</objective>
