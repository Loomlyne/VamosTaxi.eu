## Implementation Decisions

### Stripe
- **D-01:** Only the Vamos Taxi sandbox (`acct_1UIZmqHcNp9GZYjz`) is used. The old account (`…AJS2YBf21S`) is retired, and Koss disables its webhook.
- **D-02:** The Worker's `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` must be the signing secret of sandbox endpoint `we_1UIpns…`. Koss sets it; the agent never sees it.
- **D-03:** A successful payment always ends as a confirmed booking. If the booking was cancelled or expired while the customer paid, the payment **revives it as confirmed**, and the customer gets the confirmation. Refund only when the trip truly cannot run. This replaces the capture gate's silent ack (`settle.ts:163-179`).
- **D-04:** Every cancel or expire path also expires the open Stripe session (account cancel, cron expire, abandon).
- **D-05:** Refunds use the real PaymentIntent ID, never the `cs_` session ID. The sandbox charge `cs_test_a1lyA5…` (CHF 77.80, Apple Pay) gets a refund recorded in `booking_refunds`.
- **D-06:** Add a dead-letter queue consumer plus an alert. Replay the stuck `checkout.session.completed` safely.
- **D-07:** `charge.refunded` and `charge.dispute.*` reach the DB.

### Price formula (owner rule, decisions 14 and 2026-09-27 evening)
- **D-08:** Per leg: start fare + per-km × km + airport fee (airport pickup only, **added** to the start fare) + city-to-city price (when the pair matches) + every surcharge enabled in the dashboard − coupon. VAT 8.1 % on top. No minimum fare. No distance bands. No region premiums.
- **D-08a (order):** fare lines add up first; then the **coupon** (percentage) comes off, and the total **never goes below CHF 0**; then **VAT 8.1 %** is added on top. Coupon before VAT.
- **D-08b (airport fee trigger):** the airport fee applies when the pickup is an airport **or** the customer entered a flight number.
- **D-09:** A city pair covers both directions. It applies only when pickup and destination are in different places.
- **D-10:** Zones are official **city or canton boundaries** from Mapbox, so any address inside matches. Clean up the duplicate and point-of-interest zones.
- **D-10a:** Start with **all 26 Swiss cantons**. Every **canton → different canton** pair (both directions) carries **CHF 50**, the **same for Economy, Business and Van luxury** (owner-given amount, 2026-09-27). Other places are added later.
- **D-11:** Coupon caps are enforced at payment (`coupon_redemptions` is written; customer and email are passed to `evaluate_coupon`).
- **D-12:** Checkout fails closed when the price book cannot load. `pricing_live` is read, never hard-coded.
- **D-13:** Every amount comes from the dashboard. Nothing is invented. Version 18 is labelled "placeholder, not owner-approved", and Koss reviews its numbers before any publish. The agent does not click Publish.

### Payment and cancellation rules (owner, 2026-09-27 evening)
- **D-18:** A customer who pays is always confirmed (D-03).
- **D-19:** A customer who does not pay and leaves the payment page has the booking **cancelled automatically** (shipped in #57; keep it).
- **D-20 (pay link):** when the customer emails a pay link, the booking stays **locked for 24 hours**. The other person paying from the email **confirms** it. Unpaid after 24 hours, it is **cancelled automatically**.
- **D-21:** if the customer then pays it themselves on the same page, the booking is confirmed, and the pay-link recipient sees a page saying it is **already paid, no need to pay again**.
- **D-22 (race):** if both try to pay at the same time, **only one is accepted**. If both charges succeed anyway, the second one is **refunded automatically**.
- **D-23 (refunds):** a paid booking the customer cancels **more than 24 hours before pickup** gets a **full refund automatically**.
- **D-24:** cancelled **within 24 hours** of pickup: ops (the admin) must **approve** the refund and set the **percentage**.
- **D-25:** a refund asked for **after** the trip: the admin **accepts or rejects** it.

### Classes and deletes
- **D-14:** The three classes are **Economy, Business, Van luxury**. Saden → Economy, V-Class (`mercedes-benz-v-class`) → Business, Van luxury stays. First is dropped.
- **D-15:** Delete in the dashboard = hard delete when nothing references the row; otherwise hide it everywhere with a reason. `mahaha` is deleted if unreferenced, and tests use Economy.

### Admin sign-in (supersedes the earlier "MFA now" wording)
- **D-16:** There are no other staff logins; **only the admin signs in** to the dashboard. The admin is not forced to use a second factor.
- **D-16a:** In the dashboard account settings the admin chooses how to sign in, and every option must actually work end to end: add a **passkey**, add a **one-time code app (TOTP)**, or switch from **password to magic link**. When the admin has enrolled a factor, sign-in requires it (aal2); without one, password or magic link alone is accepted.
- **D-17:** Leaked-password protection is on (owner toggle in Supabase Auth). Changing the admin password or email asks the admin to sign in again first.
