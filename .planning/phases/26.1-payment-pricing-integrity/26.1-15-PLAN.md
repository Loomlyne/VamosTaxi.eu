<objective>
Make the pay link a real 24-hour hold from the moment it is sent (D-20), and give the recipient page the facts it needs for "already paid" (D-21) and "charged twice — refunded" (D-22).

Research open question 4 answered at plan time: the pay token expires at the quote lock's `exp` (`payLinkTokenExpiresAt(lockPayload.exp)` returns it unchanged), and the cron cancels on `price_snapshots.quote_lock_expires_at`. So a link sent 20 hours after the quote lives 4 hours. D-20 reads as 24 hours from sending; the UI-SPEC copy says "It was open for 24 hours". This plan implements that literal reading. **Owner sign-off point at the plan gate:** this extends the price hold for pay-link bookings and makes the charge gate trigger honour the hold.

Output: one migration + pgTAP, sender and open route changes, new refusal codes, tests.
</objective>
