# Phase 26.1 Plan 31: D-14 class line-up, second half — Summary

**Checkout shows Economy / Business / Van luxury from the dashboard name, ops vehicle and booking edits resolve the three names to the live slugs, First is gone from the public mocks, and class names stay Latin in all four languages (D-14a).**

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (plan).** `pickedClassName` and `vehicleLabel` move to `lib/checkout/picked-class.ts`. The trip rail on the trip and details steps now renders `pickedClassName(vehicle, tripForPay, t)`. It used to render `vehicleLabel(vehicle, t)`, which showed the raw live slug, e.g. `mercedes-benz-v-class` (T-26.1-89). The payment panel already used `pickedClassName(paySlug || vehicle, tripForPay, t)`. The fallback now maps live slugs too. The `classFirst`/`classVan` message keys are replaced by `classVanLuxury` in all four languages.
- **Task 2 (plan).** Fixtures: "Saden" becomes "Economy" in fleet-http and chauffeurs, and in the public-board catalog fixture ("Van" becomes "Business" there too). The two `mahaha` fixtures are converted, not deleted:
  - The unpriced leftover becomes slug `first`.
  - The inactive one becomes an inactive `saden`/"Economy" row.
  `.claude/CLAUDE.md` now names `Van luxury` and adds the D-14a Latin rule.
- **Extra 1 (26.1-27 gap).** `lib/ops/class-slug.ts` is shared by `fleet-http.ts` (`klassToSlug`/`slugToKlass`) and `bookings-write.ts` (the class edit). Two fixes:
  - A vehicle save without `vehicleClassId` and klass Economy / Business / Van luxury now finds the live class. It used to get `null`, which meant "class required".
  - An ops booking edit with one of the three names now updates `vehicle_class_id` to the live class. It used to be ignored.
  Legacy rows keep their `vehicleClassId` because the id always wins over the slug fallback.
- **Extra 2 (26.1-27 gap).**
  - `home.dc.html`: `first`/`exFirst` are removed from all four language tables and from both `names`/`ex` maps. `van` reads "Van luxury".
  - `manage-booking.dc.html` and `booking-detail.dc.html`: `CLASSES` is now the three classes on live slugs, and the default vehicle is `saden`. The dictionary's `'First'` entry is removed.
- **D-14a (coordinator, owner decision).** The Arabic values for Economy / Business / Van luxury are now Latin: two duplicate Economy/Business entries, Van luxury, and compounds such as "Economy vehicle" and "Business pax" in `app/vamos-i18n-dict.js` and `apps/web/i18n/messages/ar.json`. This follows ADR-012 (`Vamos Taxi` itself stays transliterated in Arabic in this repo; that was not touched). `class-lineup-mocks.test.ts` pins it.

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1] The plan's line reference was out of date.** The payment panel already called `pickedClassName`. The raw-slug label was on the trip rail (`vehicleLabel(vehicle, t)`). That is what changed. There is no `trip` variable in render scope, so the call is `pickedClassName(vehicle, tripForPay, t)`. The plan's literal grep `pickedClassName(vehicle, trip, t)` therefore does not match. The source pin in `picked-class.test.ts` asserts the real call.
2. **[Rule 2] `vehicleLabel` now maps the live slugs.** The fallback used to return `saden` / `mercedes-benz-v-class` / `van-luxury` verbatim whenever the trip had no class offers.
3. **Public-board leftover fixture.** The plan said to make it an *inactive* `first` row. It stays active and unpriced as slug `first`. An inactive row is dropped for a different reason, so the test would pass trivially and lose its "unpriced leftover is dropped" coverage. The other `mahaha` fixture (already inactive) became an inactive `saden`/Economy row.
4. **Test expectations changed to follow D-14 (not loosened):**
   - `fleet-http.test.ts`: klass "Business" now parses to `mercedes-benz-v-class` instead of `business`, and the legacy `van` row presents as "Van luxury" instead of "Van".
   - `ops-live-data.test.ts` still expects "Van" from `bookings-map`. That file is out of scope and was not touched.
5. **Plan grep gate `! grep -rn '"Saden"\|mahaha' apps/web/lib` still has two hits.** Both are in `ops-dc-finalize.test.ts:50,52` and are 26.1-27's *negative* pins (`not.toMatch(/mahaha/)`). They were left as they are.
6. **Extra 2 had no separate RED commit.** `class-lineup-mocks.test.ts` was written in the same commit as the fix. Its assertions fail on the base: `'First'` was present in all three pages, and the Arabic values were transliterated.

- `bookings-map.ts` labels legacy `van` as "Van". The client `cleanKlass` maps it to Van luxury.
