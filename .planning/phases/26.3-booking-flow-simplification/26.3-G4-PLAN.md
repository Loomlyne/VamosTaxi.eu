# G4 - Open Next pages (checkout, confirmation) in de/fr/ar for browser tests

## Objective
Find how a real customer's language is chosen on Next pages on vamostaxi.site, then build `openInLocale(page, path, lang)` under `apps/web/tests/support/` that reproduces it, with a spec for /de German text and /ar `dir="rtl"` at 390 with no sideways scroll.

## Tasks
1. Trace the path: `apps/web/middleware.ts`, `apps/web/i18n/routing.ts`, `apps/web/i18n/request.ts`, `apps/web/app/[locale]/layout.tsx`, `apps/web/lib/locale-shim.ts`, `app/vamos-locale.js`. Confirm with read-only curl GETs on live.
2. If a customer can reach de/fr/ar on Next pages: build helper + spec, commit.
3. If not: product bug. No helper (a helper that forces a locale a customer cannot get would prove nothing). Report evidence and the smallest fix. Do not touch middleware or i18n code.

## Constraints
Only `apps/web/tests/support/` and one new spec. No middleware/i18n edits. Live: GET only.
