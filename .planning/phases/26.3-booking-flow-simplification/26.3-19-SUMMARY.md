## Behaviour
- Extras tile label = `names[locale]`, then `names.en`, then humanised code (`extra-label.ts`). No `extraUi` map. All unticked (ticked from `extras=` on return from sign-in). Amount shows `CHF 000` while pricing is not live.
- Price: POST /api/checkout/price on class, extra or voucher change; total shows "Updating price"; total shown = `money(charged_rappen)` from the answer. `price_changed`/`class_unavailable` re-fetch extras and re-quote (max 2 tries). Coupon refusals show `couponNotFound`/`couponNoLongerValid`.
- PAY: validates class, first name, last name, e-mail, mobile, flight (airport), company name; scroll + focus + inline error + live region. Flight edited in Section 2 re-quotes on blur (airport fee depends on it). Editor open and dirty gives "Update the prices first". Intent body has no amounts (unit-tested). Redirect only to `https://checkout.stripe.com`.
- Back: `resume=` read first; trip restored from `trip_query` when the URL has none; open state refills everything, shows the info alert and scrolls to it; PAY goes to `resume.url` when trip, class, extras, voucher, contact, company and note are unchanged; otherwise the intent carries `supersedes=<booking id>` (also for expired). Expired/purged: top notice + SEE CURRENT PRICES; paid: `/confirmation/<ref>`.
- Sign-in link: `/sign-in?returnTo=` with trip, `class=`, `extras=`; voucher, company and note go to sessionStorage `vamosCheckoutReturn` (read once). Contact never stored or in a URL.
- Turnstile: a challenge on a page-body re-quote shows Alert + widget above PAY (rail and bar); the trip editor shows the widget under its error and retries with the token.
