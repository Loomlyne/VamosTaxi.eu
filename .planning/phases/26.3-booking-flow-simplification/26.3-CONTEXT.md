## Implementation Decisions

### Owner decisions given before discuss (2026-09-28, question form)
- **D-01:** Build sketch 001 Variant A on every size: home box, then ONE checkout page with
  three numbered sections (1 class with prices, 2 who is travelling, 3 payment) and one Pay
  button. The "one question per screen" variant is not used.
- **D-02:** Payment is Stripe's **hosted** Checkout page. Pay redirects to Stripe; Stripe
  returns to `/api/checkout/return`, which settles and forwards to `/confirmation/<ref>`.
  No card form inside vamostaxi.site (today `CHECKOUT_UI_MODE = "elements"`, `lib/checkout/stripe.ts:24` — this goes).
- **D-03:** 26.3 starts now; 26.2 cleanup comes after. (ROADMAP says 26.3 depends on 26.2 — superseded.)
- **D-04:** "Scratch everything" means the client: home booking box, all checkout pages,
  the payment step, the account upcoming card. Not the calculation.

### Stack and handover
- **D-05:** Home stays the DC mock (`app/home/home.dc.html`, served on `/` by middleware).
  Its booking box is simplified and hands the trip to the React checkout **in the URL**
  (e.g. `/checkout?from=…&to=…&when=…&pax=2&bags=3&flight=LX318`). The checkout gets its
  price from the server itself. No `localStorage.vamosTrip` handover; no browser draft.
- **D-06:** Home box shows **no prices at any size**. Every size: From, To, When,
  Travellers, SEE PRICES. Prices and the class choice are checkout section 1.
  (Supersedes ROADMAP 26.3 criterion 2.)
- **D-07:** Home box has no trip-type tabs; an airport pickup is detected from the From address.
- **D-08:** Flight number is asked on the **home box**, directly under From, and only when
  From is an airport. **Required** for an airport pickup. Asked once in the whole flow and
  carried to checkout in the URL; checkout shows it (editable) but does not ask again.
- **D-09:** Travellers is one button that opens two counters (Passengers − n +, Bags − n +);
  the box reads "2 passengers · 3 bags".
- **D-10:** Phone and tablet keep the same field order as desktop.

### Checkout page
- **D-11:** A class too small for the party is shown **greyed out** with "Seats up to N".
- **D-12:** Who is travelling = **one contact** (name, e-mail, phone). No "booking for someone else".
- **D-13:** Guest checkout by default, with a "Have an account? Sign in" link that returns to
  the same checkout with everything filled in and details prefilled.
- **D-14:** Extras are an **open list, all unticked**: every passenger extra in the live price
  book, with its own name and price and a tick box. No extras in the book → no section.
- **D-15:** Company receipt folded under "Need a company receipt?". Driver note folded under
  "Add a note for the driver".
- **D-16:** Voucher behind a "Have a voucher?" link that opens the field.
- **D-17:** Pay always visible: phone/tablet sticky bottom bar "Total · PAY"; desktop sticky
  summary rail on the right. Pay scrolls to the first missing field and names it.
- **D-18:** The **pay-link option is removed from checkout**. (Server pay-link routes, ops
  pay links if any, and the "No resume unless a pay-link was sent" rule stay; the planner
  checks what else still sends pay links.)
- **D-19:** Price is final before Pay: class, extras and coupon are all chosen on our page.
  Stripe shows one amount; no Stripe promotion codes.

### Stripe page and payment
- **D-20:** Stripe page shows card, Apple Pay, Google Pay **and TWINT from day one**. TWINT
  must be activated on the Stripe account (sandbox `acct_1UIZmqHcNp9GZYjz` now, live later)
  — an owner step; research confirms Checkout + TWINT requirements (CHF).
- **D-21:** **Adaptive pricing stays on**: a foreign card may pay in its own currency; Vamos
  receives CHF. The e-mail and receipt show the CHF amount **and** the amount paid in the
  customer's currency when they differ (`booking_payments.presentment_amount_minor`).
- **D-22:** Stripe page language follows the site language: en, de, fr **and ar** (verified
  2026-09-29: the Checkout Session `locale` enum includes `ar`). No English fallback needed.
- **D-42:** The code fixes the Stripe page's payment methods to exactly **card, Apple Pay,
  Google Pay, TWINT**, independent of the account's payment-method configuration. (Sandbox
  default config `pmc_1UIZnMHcNp9GZYjzEGcyOCFg` today also has PayPal, Klarna, Amazon Pay,
  Link, Bancontact, BLIK, EPS, MB Way, Pix, Satispay on; **TWINT is `available:false`,
  off** — owner must request/enable TWINT in Stripe before UAT of that button.)

### Leaving, expiry, and the unpaid row
- **D-23:** The unpaid booking row is created when Pay is clicked (price final, contact complete).
- **D-24:** Back from Stripe → checkout with everything still filled in and a line "Payment
  not finished". Pay again **reuses the same booking** (no second row).
- **D-25:** Stripe session lifetime is **30 minutes** (Stripe's minimum). An unpaid booking
  with no pay link is **deleted from the database** after it expires — not cancelled.
  (Owner, verbatim intent: "That awaiting payment will be fully deleted … she needs to book
  again.") Coming back later: checkout says the booking expired; the trip is still in the
  URL, so one click gets a fresh quote. Pay-link bookings keep their 24 h (existing rule).
  Planner: check FKs, audit rows and ops history before choosing hard delete mechanics.
- **D-26:** Ops board shows paid bookings; unpaid ones only under an "Awaiting payment" filter.

### After payment
- **D-27:** After Stripe returns: a **loading screen** until the booking is confirmed, then the
  confirmation page. After ~20 s without confirmation: "Payment received. Your confirmation
  is on its way by e-mail." Never an error screen after a successful payment.
- **D-28:** Customer-facing word for a paid booking is **"Booked"** (confirmation page,
  profile, e-mail) in en/de/fr/ar. Ops keeps its own words.
- **D-29:** Confirmation e-mail shows the **full breakdown**: fare, each ticked extra, coupon,
  VAT, total — always equal to what Stripe charged (plus presentment currency per D-21).
- **D-30:** **Remove the "Add to calendar" button** (and its route `/api/checkout/invite/<ref>`
  from the browser-navigable list if nothing else uses it).
- **D-31:** Old addresses `/checkout/trip`, `/checkout/details`, `/checkout/payment` forward
  to `/checkout`.

### Account
- **D-32:** A booking shows on the profile if it was made signed in, **or** made as a guest
  with the same e-mail the customer signs in with. (Today both paid test bookings have no
  `customer_id`.)
- **D-33:** The account page never shows a local draft as a booking (today
  `app/pages/account.dc.html` ~800–820 injects an "unpaid" card from `localStorage.vamosTrip`
  — this goes with D-05). Unpaid bookings appear only if a pay link was sent.

### Pricing (calculation unchanged except extras)
- **D-34:** A **fixed-amount coupon comes off before VAT**, as the code does today
  (`lib/checkout/payable.ts` D-08a; `lib/pricing/policy.ts:202`). Example the owner signed:
  CHF 100.00 net, CHF 10 coupon → (100 − 10) × 1.081 = CHF 97.29. Do not change it.
- **D-35:** **Generic extras.** An extra is any passenger surcharge the owner adds in the
  dashboard under any name. Off by default; the customer ticks; a ticked extra is priced by
  the server from its price-book row, charged, and saved on the booking so ops sees it.
  Never fix by matching or renaming a code. Every hard-coded `child_seat` /
  `oversized_luggage` / `extra_stop` path is a defect (list in code_context).

### Pickup time and test data
- **D-36:** New bookings store the correct instant: `scheduled_local` interpreted in
  `Europe/Zurich` → UTC `scheduled_at` (today `lock-to-rpc.ts:60` copies the wall clock).
  Reminder, 24 h refund rule, cancel and no-show sweeps then run on time.
- **D-37:** **Delete all 33 bookings** now in the live database — the owner says they are all
  his tests (6 paid, all on the Stripe sandbox). The plan ships ONE SQL script that first
  copies every affected row (bookings, legs, payments, refunds, tickets, e-mails, audit,
  anything with a FK) into a backup schema/table, then deletes. **The owner runs it himself**
  after the new flow is live and his new test booking works. The agent never runs it.
  `CLAUDE.local.md` "real paid bookings, never wipe" stays the rule for every booking made
  after this — this is a one-off, owner-approved exception.
