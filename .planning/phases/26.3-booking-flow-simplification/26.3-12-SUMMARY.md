# Phase 26.3 Plan 12: Unpaid deletion wiring Summary

Unpaid web bookings are hard-deleted (D-25) by the session-expired webhook and by an hourly sweep. Both retrieve every Stripe session of the booking and delete only when all are expired and unpaid (`allSessionsExpiredUnpaid`). No mailer is imported (D-45).

## Commits
- 423b93db feat(26.3-12): Stripe-verified unpaid purge (webhook helper and hourly sweep)
- ceab54ac feat(26.3-12): purge on session-expired webhook; refund and alert a paid session with no booking

## What changed
- `purge-unpaid.ts`: sweep uses `purge_candidates('35 minutes')`, skips on any open session, reports (emit) and skips on a paid or complete session, skips a booking on a Stripe error and continues, counts `purge_unpaid_booking = false` as skipped. Empty session list is never purged. Webhook helper never purges `metadata.kind = "extra"` and uses `checkout_booking_session_ids`.
- `worker.ts`: hourly branch calls the sweep first, in its own try/catch, emitting `purge_unpaid` counts only, then `expireUnpaidBookings` (now pay-link-only in SQL).
- `settle.ts`: new `purgeOnSessionExpired` dep, called after the payment row is marked failed on `checkout.session.expired`; failure is logged and acked. P0002 on a paid, non-extra session with a PaymentIntent refunds the whole charge (Stripe reason `requested_by_customer`, metadata `vamos_reason: "booking_missing"`), calls `alertStuckPayment`, records the event settled and acks. Refund failure still alerts and acks.
- `stripe.ts createRefund`: `amountRappen` may be null (full refund); optional extra metadata.

## Tests
- purge-unpaid.test.ts (12) and settle.test.ts (new 7 cases, 45 total) green; stripe.test.ts, expire-unpaid.test.ts green. tsc: no errors in my files; two remaining errors are in the confirmation page (other plans mid-edit).

## Deviations
1. [Rule 2] `purgeOnSessionExpired(deps, sessionId, bookingId)` takes the booking id as third argument (from the settle row) because `vamos_system` table grants for a session-to-booking lookup were not confirmed; avoids a new grant.
2. [Rule 2] The P0002 refund only fires when the session is at least 10 minutes old. A P0002 on a young session can just mean the intent transaction has not committed yet, and refunding then would refund a real customer. Unknown age means retry. Young or unpaid or extra sessions keep the old retry behaviour.
3. [Rule 3] Added `purgeOnSessionExpired` to the two fake deps in `tests/integration/webhook-replay.spec.ts` (required by the new SettleDeps field).

## Residual to note

- Expired-event P0002 (payment row already purged, duplicate event) still retries as before.
