## Happens by itself after deploy

a) **VT-26-0733 and VT-26-0734 are deleted by the hourly clean-up.** They are the only pending bookings on live: no pay link, one Stripe session each, nothing captured. Their sessions were created on 28 Sep with a 24 h window, so Stripe reports them expired; the purge runs only when every session is expired and unpaid (`apps/web/lib/checkout/purge-unpaid.ts`, `app.purge_eligible` in `20260930110000_purge_unpaid_booking.sql`). No e-mail is sent; one audit line without personal data stays.

Note: both trips were for 29 Sep 00:40 and 01:25 Zurich time, already past, so these are "Booked" e-mails for past test trips, to the owner's own address.

c) The child seat's German, French and Arabic names appear only after the owner opens it in the dashboard and presses Save (UAT step 13); until then those languages show "Child seat".

d) A dashboard phone booking where "Take card" opened a Stripe page and nobody paid is deleted about 35 minutes later, like any unpaid booking without a pay link. A phone booking with no Stripe page opened is never deleted.
