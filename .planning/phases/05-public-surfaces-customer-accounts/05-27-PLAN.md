<objective>
Close every blocking finding on Phase 5 PR #15 without widening product scope: bind each
Turnstile verification to the contact action and a configured public hostname, reserve durable
outbox state transitions for the Worker-only database identity, repair the served static/DC
contact lifecycle and headers, preserve locale, and prove the actual served routes behave
truthfully.

Purpose: no browser caller can forge delivery acceptance; a `success: true` Turnstile record
for another action/hostname cannot write a contact row; and the production-served DC contact
form remains usable after delayed script loading, retry and another-message reset.

Output: a narrow remediation commit series on `phase-5`, new local proofs, staging-only UAT,
then renewed independent/Cursor/security review. PR #15 is not merged or production-deployed
by this plan.
</objective>

<context>
This is remediation following independent review, Codex/PR review and the non-approved Cursor
result on PR #15. It deliberately changes only contact delivery/security and its static/DC
served route.

Locked security facts:
- `verifyTurnstile` currently sends `action=contact` but accepts Siteverify solely on
  `success === true`; it does not bind Siteverify's returned `action` or `hostname`.
- `claim_contact_delivery` and `finalize_contact_delivery` are `SECURITY DEFINER`, but their
  EXECUTE grants currently include `anon` and `authenticated`. A caller knowing a submission
  UUID can claim and forge accepted outbox state.
- Hyperdrive authenticates as `vamos_edge`; Phase 7's pre-existing `vamos_system` nologin role
  is reachable only by that Worker login through `SET ROLE`. It is the least-privileged
  server-only identity for outbox RPC execution; no service-role key or browser credential is
  added.

Locked public facts remain unchanged:
- `info@vamostaxi.site`, `+41 79 626 70 82`, `wa.me/41796267082`.
- Existing contact copy only says accepted for delivery/review, never that a customer copy was
  sent.
- Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok owner-provided destinations stay as already approved;
  this plan does not revisit them.

Release fences remain unchanged: no live DNS, no production deployment, no secrets in source,
and no merge before clean review/CI plus configured staging Turnstile/Resend and real inbox
replay evidence.
</context>
