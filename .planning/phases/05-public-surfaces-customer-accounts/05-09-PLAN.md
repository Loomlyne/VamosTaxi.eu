<objective>
Render the second and third legal pages through the shell plan 05-04 built. This plan adds
content and its proofs; it adds no layout.

Privacy (389 mock lines, 19 TBC pills) and cookies (383 lines, 14 pills) are the two pages with
the highest density of values the owner still owes us — retention periods, an analytics provider
name, a supervisory-authority contact. Law 04's whole point is that each of those stays a
labelled gap rather than becoming a stated fact on a live policy page.

Purpose: SITE-05 asks for real numbers where the owner supplied them and labelled TBC pills
where not. These two pages are where "where not" is most of the page.
Output: `/privacy`, `/cookies`, and 32 four-language baselines.
</objective>
