<objective>
Render the last two legal pages, and satisfy I18N-08 for real.

Every other page in this phase satisfies I18N-08 by being genuinely four-language. Imprint is
the one page where the requirement bites: the mock claims four languages, its content is
bilingual by design ("Deutsch ist die verbindliche Fassung"), and the Phase 1 migration has
since produced unreviewed French and Arabic renderings of its English half. A page in that state
must say what it is, which is exactly what the requirement asks for and what plan 05-03's
registry and notice were built to do.

Cancellation is the other high-TBC page: 20 pills covering refund percentages and windows, none
of which the owner has confirmed. An invented percentage on a refund policy is a promise the
business would be held to.

Purpose: SITE-05 and I18N-08 in the same pass, on the two pages where getting them wrong is
most expensive.
Output: `/cancellation`, `/imprint`, and 32 four-language baselines including the notice.
</objective>
