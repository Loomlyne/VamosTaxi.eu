<objective>
Server half of owner decision D-03a: a sign-up is accepted only with the tick, and the tick is
recorded in 26.5's `account_agreement_records` table (surface `sign-up`), never in `consent_log`.

Recording moment (the 26.5 rule, read from `apps/web/app/api/checkout/intent/route.ts` and
`lib/checkout/intent.ts`): 26.5 writes the record at the ticked submit (the PAY press), before the
account exists, and an account is only ever made when a record exists. It does NOT wait for e-mail
confirmation (26.5 D-10 withdrew that wording). Sign-up follows the same rule: the row is written
at the ticked submit of the sign-up form, inside POST /api/auth, after validation and before the
Supabase call. No record, no account request.

Role: the sign-up request is anonymous and the auth route has no database identity today. 26.5 D-19
forbids anon and authenticated. The write runs as `vamos_system` through `asSystem` (the same
wrapper the public contact form uses for its definer functions). The grant goes to `vamos_system`
only.

Metadata flag: `signup_consent: "pending"` is REMOVED. With the record written at submit it carries
nothing, nothing reads it, and "pending" would stay untrue for ever. Users who already carry it keep
it untouched.

Purpose: META-03 stays about cookies only (D-01); the agreement to open an account has its own record.
Output: one grant migration, pgTAP, a Worker-client DB test, the server check and write, updated unit
tests and auth e2e script.
</objective>
