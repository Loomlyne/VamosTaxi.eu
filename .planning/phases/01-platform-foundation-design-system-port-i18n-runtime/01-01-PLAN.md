<objective>
Lay the single end-to-end path this whole phase expands from: a pnpm monorepo whose
`apps/web` Next.js 15 application builds through `@opennextjs/cloudflare` into one Cloudflare
Worker that exports `fetch`, `scheduled` and `queue`, serves `staging.vamostaxi.eu` over a real
custom domain, renders the right language server-side from a `[locale]` route segment, and paints
a ported design-system component whose CSS is already in the HTML before hydration.

Purpose: every later plan in this phase — the CI gates, the 33-component port, the dictionary
migration, the currency store — is an expansion of one of the four layers this slice touches. If
the architecture is wrong (SSR i18n flashes English, component CSS arrives after hydration, the
Worker cannot carry three handlers), it is wrong here, after one commit, not after ten.

Output: a deployed staging Worker whose raw HTTP response already proves German copy, Arabic
direction and design-system CSS without JavaScript.
</objective>

  <context>
    D-11 and D-12 are locked and are NOT being re-opened: one dynamic `[lang]` segment, English at
    the root, German/French/Arabic prefixed. What still needs a human signature is the concrete
    contract that gets published the moment `staging.vamostaxi.eu` goes up — because Phase 11's
    Freshpage redirect map, every `hreflang` alternate and any link indexed before then are all
    written against it. CONTEXT.md rates this one-way: changing it after launch needs a redirect
    migration, not a refactor.

    The one detail D-12 leaves genuinely open is how `/en/<path>` behaves. D-12 says "a rewrite
    keeps `/en/…` from becoming duplicate content"; `next-intl`'s `localePrefix: 'as-needed'`
    ships a redirect rather than a rewrite by default. Both avoid duplicate content; they publish
    different contracts.
  </context>
  <options>
    <option id="option-a">
      <name>Redirect: /en/about responds 308 to /about (recommended, next-intl default)</name>
      <pros>Exactly one canonical URL per page, which is what the Phase 11 redirect map and the sitemap both want. No configuration fighting the library. A shared or bookmarked /en/ link self-corrects.</pros>
      <cons>An extra round trip for anyone who hand-types the /en/ form. Marginally different from the literal word "rewrite" in D-12.</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="option-b">
      <name>Rewrite: /en/about serves /about's content at the /en/ URL with a canonical link tag</name>
      <pros>Matches D-12's literal wording. No redirect hop.</pros>
      <cons>Two live URLs for every page, deduplicated only by a canonical tag search engines may or may not honour. Doubles what the Phase 11 redirect map has to reason about.</cons>
    </option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Select: option-a or option-b</resume-signal>
