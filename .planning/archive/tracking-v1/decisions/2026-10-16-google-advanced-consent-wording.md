## The risk he accepted (recorded, not reopened)

- EDÖB guide v1.1 (06.10.2025) §3.13.1: "data processing must not be activated by default when the website is
  accessed". It recommends a two-click solution for third-party services and tracking pixels. §1.1 counts pixels as
  "similar technologies". The owner was told this and accepts the risk (`2026-10-16-tracking-before-consent.md`).
- Also new since then: Meta's Business Tools Terms (§3 d, effective 3 Nov 2025,
  https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/businesstools) say: "In jurisdictions that require informed consent for
  storing and accessing cookies (such as but not limited to the European Union) you must ensure, in a verifiable
  manner, that an end user provides all necessary consents before you use Meta Business Tools to enable the storage
  of and access to Meta cookies…". Switzerland is not named. Swiss law usually requires an opt-out, not prior
  consent, except for high-risk cases (EDÖB §3.10.5). Visitors from the EU who open the site fall under this clause
  in any case. This is a contract risk with Meta, on top of the EDÖB one.

## A. Google advanced

**What changes:** gtag.js loads on the same pages for every visitor, with every consent signal denied. It sets no
cookie. It sends signals without cookies: time, browser, page address (path only), the previous site's name,
consent state, and "came from a Google advert: yes/no". With defaults 1 and 2 it also sends the button and step
names and, for a paid booking, the amount. Accept grants as today, from `/api/consent/state`. Google gets the IP
address when its script loads, from every visitor. Google: "While consent is denied, the Google tags send
measurements without cookies" (developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/concepts/consent-mode, updated
2026-07-30).

### D3. Consent version

`CONSENT_POLICY_VERSION` moves from `2026-10-13` to **the ship day** (Zurich date). Its pin in `legal-gate.test.ts`
moves with it.

What that does to visitors who already chose:
- `/api/consent/state` reads choices under the current version only. On their next page load **everyone** gets
  `chosen: false` and sees the banner again, whether they accepted or refused before.
- Until they choose again, they get what a refuser gets: Google denied (signals without cookies), Meta page views
  with its cookies, no Meta events, no Tag Manager. Anyone who had accepted gets no Google cookies, no Meta events
  and no Meta server events until they accept again.
- A booking paid after the bump by someone who has not chosen again sends no Meta server event and no granted
  Google conversion. Under default 1 it sends only the cookieless Google ping (amount and CHF).
- Old choices stay in `consent_log` as proof. No migration, no deletion.
