# Owner decision: the Meta wording (2026-09-30, 01:00 +04)

Given through the question form in the control session. His answer, verbatim option:
"This is my text, all three, all four languages".

## What this changes

Phase 26 decisions D-03 and D-06 and requirement META-01 said that only text the owner pastes
opens the Meta gate, and that a sentence or translation written by an agent keeps it shut. An
earlier session refused to draft the lines. `CLAUDE.local.md` says "Do not invent Meta legal
lines".

On 2026-09-30 the owner asked the control session to draft them, was shown the rule and the
conflict, read the draft, and adopted it as his own text in all four languages. From this
decision on, the texts below are the owner's lines for META-01. No lawyer has read them.

The gate still opens only in code, by the phase that ships it, and only when all three texts
are live on the banner, the cookies page and the privacy page in all four languages
(Phase 26 D-05, D-19). Nothing else about the gate changes: a new consent policy version,
an old Accept does not count (META-02, D-14).

## Left out on purpose

Statute article numbers and the legal mechanism for processing abroad. The adviser gaps for
those stay. The text states what the site sends, not what Meta does with it.

## Two facts to keep true

- Company: Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.
- The cookies `_fbp` and `_fbc` are kept for up to 90 days. If the build sets another lifetime,
  the text and the build must be made to agree before shipping.
