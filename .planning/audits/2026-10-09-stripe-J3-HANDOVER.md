## What changed, per finding

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| W4-07, W5-02 | `checkout_payment_settle` and `checkout_extra_payment_settle` take four new optional trailing arguments (`p_amount_total_minor`, `p_amount_currency`, `p_amount_received_minor`, `p_metadata_booking_id`; null skips that check). The Worker (`settle.ts`, `chargeFactsFromSession`) sends the session's `amount_total` and `currency`, the expanded PaymentIntent's `amount_received` (only when it is `succeeded` and in the session's currency) and `metadata.booking_id`. Mismatch = currency not CHF, amount_total or amount_received not the row's `charged_rappen` (for a difference: the extra price record's total), or a metadata booking id that is neither the booking id nor its quote id. **Behaviour on a mismatch (main payment):** the capture is recorded (`succeeded`, `captured_at`, FX as usual), a `payment.succeeded` event with payload `amount_mismatch`, `reasons`, `expected_rappen`, `amount_total_minor`, `amount_received_minor`, `currency` is written, the booking stays `pending` (not confirmed, not revived, not refunded, no confirmation mail, no account), the Stripe event stays open with `last_error = 'amount_mismatch'`, the other open pages are expired, and the Worker mails info@ (new kind `amount-mismatch`, ids only: event id, type, object id, booking reference, no name/address/amount) and only then closes the event. A mail that fails keeps the event open and retries (the settle answers the same again, nothing is written twice). **For a difference payment:** the capture is recorded and the change is NOT applied; it takes the existing not-applied path (Refund due, the existing info@ mail) with event `refund.requested` `via: amount_mismatch` and the received facts. **Adaptive pricing:** Stripe keeps the Checkout Session and PaymentIntent in the integration currency (CHF) when it presents EUR/USD/AED (docs read 2026-10-09; the presented amount rides in `presentment_details`, which `fxFromSession` already maps to the FX columns), so a legitimate presentment is never a mismatch (test 5, pgTAP). Sessions made before 2025-03-31 with the old `currency_conversion` shape: the creation currency and total of `currency_conversion` are used and the PaymentIntent is not read. The old 10- and 9-argument functions are dropped (defaults on the new ones would make a call ambiguous); an older Worker's call resolves to the new function with no amount checks. |
| W4-08 | Duplicate detection also looks across the booking: another succeeded payment on the same booking is a duplicate even with a different snapshot. A difference payment (named by `booking_edit_requests.extra_payment_id`) is not counted. |
| W5-03 | `tg_payment_update_whitelist`: status moves only forward: `requires_payment` to succeeded/failed/canceled/duplicate; `failed` or `canceled` to succeeded/failed/canceled/duplicate (a late payment; `failed` and `canceled` may swap because Stripe sends `checkout.session.expired` and `payment_intent.canceled` for one page in either order, which J2's late-event guard also relies on); `duplicate` and `succeeded` terminal; nothing returns to `requires_payment`; a row reaching succeeded/duplicate needs `captured_at`, a failed/canceled row must have none. |
| W5-09 | `compute_cancellation_refund` excludes `duplicate` payments from the basis; `app.booking_paid_net` excludes them and the refunds recorded against them. `ops_cancel_booking` and `app.apply_customer_cancel` take the owed amount from `compute_cancellation_refund` and keep an "anything captured" flag, so they needed no change. Test: a duplicate plus a cancel more than 24 h ahead owes the booking's own payment only. |
| W5-10 | `tg_payment_matches_snapshot` skips its two clock checks (payment window, quote lock) for one succeeded insert with `captured_at` when the transaction-local flag `vamos.settle_capture` is on; `checkout_extra_payment_settle` sets it around its one insert and resets it. Amount, booking and snapshot-binding checks still run. A difference paid after its price record expired is recorded and applied. Tests: the flag is off again afterwards; the gate still refuses an ordinary insert, and a flagged insert that is not a captured succeeded row. |
| (support) | `checkout_expire_unpaid` (body of `20260930110000`) no longer cancels a pending booking that holds a captured payment (a held mismatch); test bookings are unchanged. |

## Deviations

- W5-03: the audit proposed `failed` and `canceled` to `{succeeded}` only. I also allow `failed` and `canceled` to swap and to become `duplicate`, because the normal expiry sequence sends both events in either order and would otherwise raise in settle.
- W4-07: "record the payment as needing review with last_error 'amount_mismatch'" is implemented on the Stripe event (`stripe_events.last_error`, which exists) plus the payment event payload; `booking_payments` has no error column and its update whitelist is deliberately narrow. There is no new payment status.
- W5-10: the audit offered "create the row at page creation" or a flag; I took the flag, limited to the two clock checks of the gate.
- The DE/FR/AR strings of the new ops mail are mine.

## Found, not fixed

- A held mismatch has no dashboard list: ops sees it through the info@ mail, the `payment.succeeded` event payload on the booking timeline, and a `pending` booking with a succeeded payment. A "needs review" queue on the dashboard is a UI job.
- A held main-payment mismatch leaves the customer on a `pending` booking after paying; what the return page and the customer's manage page say about it is J5/J8 territory.
- A duplicate charge is refunded for `charged_rappen` (the expected amount), not for what Stripe actually collected; if a mismatching amount also arrives as a duplicate Stripe may refuse the refund (it retries, then the DLQ mail and the sweep report it).
- `captured_at`/status consistency is enforced only by the trigger on changes, not by a CHECK (needs the live read in step 5 of the deploy notes).
- `checkout_abandon_unpaid` / `checkout_requote_cancel` can still cancel a pending booking that holds a captured payment (only the hourly clock cancel was guarded).
- The old `currency_conversion` session shape (before 2025-03-31) is handled from the type docs, not from a real old session.
- `apps/web/tests/integration/*.spec.ts` (Playwright) were type-checked only.
