## The eleven checks

1. **Settle identity is the 14-argument form everywhere it is cast.** The running function is `20261009115000:172-187` (four optional amount arguments after the ten FX arguments). `110000:258` drops the previous identity before creating the 14-argument form, so a 10-type cast has no target. Casts that name the function use the 14-type identity: `db_privilege_hardening.test.sql:180`, `:236-237`; `refund_lock_order.test.sql:83-84`; `amount_state_verify.test.sql:320`; `settle_never_stuck.test.sql:364` and `settle_second_delivery_processed.test.sql:141` (same identity with `bigint`). The Worker calls it positionally with all 14 (`settle.ts:832-846`). The extra settle is the 13-argument form (`settle.ts:814-827` matches `110000:995-1009`). `database.types.ts:3371-3386` lists the same 14 names, including the four optional amount arguments. Calls that pass fewer arguments are legal: the amount arguments have defaults. They are not casts.

2. **Settle refuses non-CHF, an amount mismatch, and a booking that is not payable; a duplicate is not a second charge.** In `115000`: currency other than CHF, `amount_total` or `amount_received` different from `charged_rappen`, or session metadata that is neither the booking id nor the quote id, all add a reason (`:527-545`). On any reason the capture is recorded, the booking is not confirmed, not revived and not refunded, and the event stays open with `last_error = 'amount_mismatch'` (`:547-591`). An erased booking raises `P0002` (`:512-515`). `is_test` captures and marks `refund_required:test_booking` (`:594-629`). A cancelled booking, or a pending booking whose lock and pay-link hold are both past, goes to revive or refund (`:651-826`), never a second confirm of a live pending booking. A second succeeded payment on the same snapshot, or any other main payment of the booking, is `duplicate` with `refund_required:duplicate_charge` and `already_settled = true` (`:452-507`). `checkout_attach_payment` refuses erased, test, and any status other than `pending` or `quote` (`160000:500-504`). The Worker settles from a re-read session's `payment_status` (`settle.ts:221-224`), and the return route does the same (`return-settle.ts:151-163`, `:159-179`).
