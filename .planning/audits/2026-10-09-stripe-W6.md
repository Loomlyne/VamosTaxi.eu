## Checked and clean

Refund amount cap, currency, partial, idempotency
- Per payment cap in SQL, `charged_rappen - sum(booking_refunds)`, an exact amount above it raises `refund-exceeds-remaining`, a percent is `least(left, round(charged*percent/100))`, nothing is planned when left <= 0 (`20261007280000_refund_lock_order.sql:268-318`). TS mirrors it before the call (`apps/web/lib/ops/refund.ts:305-350`) and the percent/amount parser rejects 0, negatives, non-integers, percent >100, percent with amount (`refund-map.ts:168-221`).
- Currency: `charged_rappen` is the CHF figure; refund is made on the PaymentIntent in rappen (`stripe.ts:230-255`); a non-CHF refund coming back from Stripe is refused in the webhook (`money-events.ts:158`).
- One open intent per payment (`booking_refund_intents_open_payment_uq`, `20261005140000:416-418`), a second press with open intents returns them as `resumed` and plans nothing new (`20261007280000:132-153`); intents carry the Stripe key `refund-intent:<id>` (stable), a retry after a Stripe refusal uses `<key>:<attempts>` only when the last error is `stripe:` and first looks the refund up by `metadata.vamos_intent` (`refund.ts:425-447`, `stripe.ts:268-281`), so a 24 h key expiry cannot make a second refund.
- Concurrent double press: both calls end up on the same intent and the same Stripe key; `ops_refund_intent_sent` is idempotent on `stripe_refund_id` (unique on `booking_refunds` and `booking_refund_intents`) and takes payment, then booking, then intent locks (`20261007280000:379-395`). A Stripe success followed by a database failure leaves the intent open as `unrecorded`, never `failed` (`refund.ts:474-498`); `charge_already_refunded` voids the intent (`refund.ts:458-460`).
- Admin gate: `withAdmin` (admin only, active staff row re-checked, Origin allowlist, document navigation refused) on refund, refund-decision, GET picker (`staff-json.ts:64-120`); `ops_refund_decide` checks `app.is_admin()` itself (`20261005140000:1114`); refund and decline events carry the actor (`booking_events` rows `refund.issued`, `refund.declined`).
- Full-tier rule (cancelled > 24 h ahead: 100 % only, no decline) enforced in TS and SQL (`refund.ts:335-338`, `20261007280000:331-336`, `20261005140000:1141-1146`).

App refund versus `charge.refunded`
- App-made refunds carry `vamos_source=app`, `booking_id`, `payment_id` metadata (`stripe.ts:240-246`); the webhook never records them itself, raises `app_refund_pending` and retries after 30 s until the app row exists, then it is a no-op on `stripe_refund_id` (`20260928120000:99-157`, `money-events.ts:197-199`). No double record in either arrival order. The charge is re-read from Stripe, refunds are paged past 10 (`stripe.ts:282-310`).

Cancellation and expiry
- Customer paid cancel (signed in): CSRF Origin, write rate limit (fail-closed), session email must equal `contact_email` under RLS, key format check (`paid-cancel.ts:268-296`, `app/api/account/bookings/paid-cancel/route.ts`). Guest cancel: token is SHA-256 of 32 random bytes, `purpose = 'manage'`, not revoked, not expired, the page's booking must equal the cookie's booking (`wrong-booking`), one transaction (`paid-cancel.ts:233-262`, `20261005100000:42-51`). Pay-link tokens (`purpose='pay'`) cannot cancel.
- A cancel never calls Stripe for a refund; it ends waiting change requests and closes their Stripe pages first (`paid-cancel.ts:130-176`).
- Unpaid expiry/cancel: DB cancel first, then every open session is expired one by one, failures logged and skipped (`expire-unpaid.ts:30-52`, `cancel-unpaid.ts:35-45`, `bookings-write.ts:178-199`); a late payment on a cancelled booking is revived or refunded by settle (design D-04, belongs to W4). `lock-expire` needs the booking's manage cookie or the open session id, CSRF and rate limit (`lock-expire.ts:52-76`, route).
- Purge cannot delete a paid or disputed booking: `app.purge_eligible` requires pending, no pay link, no succeeded/captured payment, no refund, no dispute, no notification, no edit request, no review (`20260930110000:21-43`); the Worker also requires every Stripe session to be retrieved and `expired` and `unpaid` (`stripe.ts:366-384`, `purge-unpaid.ts:28-65`, `intent.ts:315-330`); a paid session seen is reported, never deleted around (`purge-unpaid.ts:56-63`).
