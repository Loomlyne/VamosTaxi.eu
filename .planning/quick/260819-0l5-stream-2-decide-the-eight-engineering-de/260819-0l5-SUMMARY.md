  line* of the `**Answer:**` paragraph (an `awk` pattern that prints on match and exits). My

  citation or the owner-must-answer marker sits on the answer's first line, before any wrap —
  matching what the plan's own automated gate actually checks, while keeping the full prose
  argument in the lines that follow.
- **Files modified:** `docs/build/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`
- **Commit:** `a557119` (Q8), `364e45f` (the remaining nine)
