Logo Construction
The Vamos Taxi logo is a refined typographic logo that
combines modernity, mobility symbolism, and digital identity,
reflecting the brand’s mission of elegant, tech-driven
transportation.

Typography & Structure : The logo is based on a custom
sans-serif typeface, rounded and geometric, reflecting
approachability, comfort, and modernity.

Each letter is perfectly spaced, with a harmonious rhythm that
reinforces simplicity and professionalism.

The overall composition maintains a horizontal alignment,
ideal for both digital and physical branding applications.
                                                       Iconic Modifications
                                                       Letter “V”:
                                                       Constructed with a clean vertical stem and a soft curve.
                                                       Topped with three yellow checkered pixels, referencing:
                                                       The classic visual identity of traditional taxis (checkerboard pattern).
                                                       The digital transformation of transport (pixels = tech/data).
                                                       A visual “spark” symbolizing energy, start, or ignition.
                                                       Letter “t”:
                                                       Modified to include a yellow horizontal bar, evoking:
                                                       A road marking or directional line, suggesting forward motion.
                                                       The color yellow emphasizes energy, speed, and visibility.




Safety Margin
A minimum clear space surrounds the brand
logo to ensure maximum visibility and prevent
any interference from surrounding text or graphic
elements.
This buffer zone protects the integrity of the logo
across all applications and must be respected at all
times unless otherwise specified.

   About Qurova :

   Qurova is a sleek, geometric sans-serif
   typeface known for its modern curves,
   balanced proportions, and futuristic simplicity.

   perfect for a brand like Vamos Taxi that values
   elegance, innovation, and clarity.

   Modern & Stylish

   that project a modern and upscale image.
