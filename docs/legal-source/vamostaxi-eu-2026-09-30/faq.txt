When will I receive my booking confirmation?

After completing the booking process and finalizing your reservation, a confirmation email will be sent to the email address provided. This email will contain details of your itinerary, including information on when to expect the driver's details.

What is the cancellation policy?

At Vamos Taxi, we understand that unexpected events can arise, leading to changes in your travel plans. That's why we offer you the flexibility to cancel your transfer services. You can cancel your transfer services up to 24 hours before your scheduled service for a full refund.

Please note that refunds must be processed by a customer service agent. Therefore, if you need to cancel your booking, we kindly ask you to contact our customer service team using the link provided below. They will assist you with the cancellation process.

Is it necessary to tip the chauffeur?

Prices displayed on our website include gratuity for all our chauffeurs worldwide, ensuring you receive world-class service with every ride you book.

While tipping is included, as our guest, you have the freedom to provide additional gratuity to your chauffeur in cash on-site, which would be greatly appreciated. Tipping is a wonderful gesture that can enhance your experience and show gratitude for exceptional service.

We recognize that each guest's financial circumstances vary, so tipping your chauffeur is entirely at your discretion. Any gratuity received by our chauffeurs contributes to their commitment to providing excellent customer service and ensuring your utmost satisfaction.

How do I get in touch with my chauffeur?

At Vamos Taxi, we recognize the importance of providing driver details well in advance of your scheduled pickup time. To ensure your peace of mind, we dispatch driver information via both SMS and email, precisely 6 hours prior to your designated pickup time.

Should you require expedited access to driver details before the standard 6-hour window, we invite you to reach out to our dedicated customer support team at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled pickup time. We are committed to accommodating your needs and ensuring a seamless experience.

Am I allowed to bring my own child/booster seat?

Yes, you are welcome to bring your own child/booster seat for added comfort and safety during your ride with us. We prioritize the well-being of our passengers and want you to feel secure throughout your journey.
