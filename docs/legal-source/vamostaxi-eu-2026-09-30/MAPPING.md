## C. Gaps the old site does not answer

Extra stop fee, oversize item fee, modification deadline, refund decision days,
no-show call attempts (the old text says contact "at least twice" for the customer, not for
the company), cookie durations, DPO or "not required", EU representative, the processor
regions, photography and agency credits, the two adviser notes on the privacy page
(statutory references, transfer mechanism).

## D. What the old privacy page does not cover either

Automatic guest accounts and sign-in by e-mail link (Phase 26.5). Its §5 covers a customer
account opened by the customer. The paragraph for 26.5 is drafted separately for the
owner's approval.
